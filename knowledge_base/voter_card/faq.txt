
1. Question: What is a Voter ID Card in India?
Answer: A Voter ID Card, also called EPIC (Elector's Photo Identity Card), is an official identity document issued by the Election Commission of India that allows eligible citizens to vote in elections.

2. Question: What is Form 6?
Answer: Form 6 is the official application form used for enrollment of a new voter in the electoral roll for the first time.

3. Question: Who can apply for a Voter ID Card?
Answer: Any Indian citizen who is 18 years of age or above on the qualifying date (usually January 1st of the year) can apply for a Voter ID Card.

4. Question: What is the minimum age required to apply for Voter ID?
Answer: You must be 18 years of age or older on the qualifying date to register as a voter.

5. Question: Is there any fee for applying for a Voter ID Card?
Answer: No. The Voter ID Card is issued free of cost and delivered through speed post under proper acknowledgment.

6. Question: Where should I submit Form 6?
Answer: Form 6 should be submitted to the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) of the Assembly Constituency (AC) or Parliamentary Constituency (PC) where you ordinarily reside.

7. Question: Can I apply online for a Voter ID Card?
Answer: Yes. You can apply online through the official National Voters' Service Portal (NVSP) at www.nvsp.in.

8. Question: What documents are required for Voter ID registration?
Answer: You need proof of age (birth certificate, 10th mark sheet, passport, etc.) and proof of address (Aadhaar card, electricity bill, ration card, passport, etc.).

9. Question: Is Aadhaar Card mandatory for Voter ID registration?
Answer: Aadhaar is not mandatory but providing your Aadhaar number helps in authentication and faster processing of your application.

10. Question: What photograph specifications are required for Form 6?
Answer: A recent good quality passport size unsigned colour photograph (4.5cm X 3.5cm) with white background. Eyes must be open and both edges of face must be clearly visible.

11. Question: Can I fill Form 6 in Hindi or my regional language?
Answer: Yes. You can fill Form 6 either in English or in the official language of your state. This will not be a ground for rejection.

12. Question: What if I don't know my Assembly Constituency or Parliamentary Constituency number?
Answer: You can get assistance from the Electoral Registration Officer. Your application will not be rejected for not mentioning the AC/PC number.

13. Question: What documents can I use as proof of age?
Answer: Birth certificate, school certificate (10th mark sheet), PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport, driving license, or any government-issued document showing date of birth.

14. Question: What documents can I use as proof of address?
Answer: Aadhaar card, electricity/water/gas bill (at least 1 year old), passport, bank passbook, ration card, rent agreement, sale deed, or land owning records.

15. Question: Can I use my parents' address proof document?
Answer: Yes. You can attach a self-attested copy of address proof in the name of your parents, spouse, or adult child if they are already enrolled as electors at the same address.

16. Question: What if I don't have any of the mentioned address proof documents?
Answer: You should enclose any other document as proof and mention its name in the declaration. Field verification will be conducted by the ERO.

17. Question: Can homeless persons apply for Voter ID?
Answer: Yes. Homeless Indian citizens living in sheds/pavements can be enrolled through field verification if they are otherwise eligible.

18. Question: Can students apply for Voter ID at their hostel address?
Answer: Yes. Eligible students can be enrolled either at their parent's place or at the hostel/mess where they are ordinarily residing.

19. Question: What should a married woman mention in the form?
Answer: A married female applicant may preferably mention her husband's name in the 'Name of Relative' section.

20. Question: What are the gender options in Form 6?
Answer: The gender options are Male, Female, and Third Gender. You must tick mark the appropriate box.

21. Question: Can Third Gender applicants choose their gender?
Answer: Yes. Applicants belonging to Third Gender may indicate their gender as Male, Female, or Third Gender.

22. Question: What is the Declaration section in Form 6?
Answer: The Declaration section contains statements you must confirm, including your citizenship, ordinary residence, and that you are not enrolled elsewhere. Giving false statements is punishable.

23. Question: What is the penalty for making false statements in Form 6?
Answer: Making false statements is punishable under Section 31 of the Representation of People Act, 1950, with imprisonment up to one year or fine or both.

24. Question: How long does it take to receive a Voter ID Card after applying?
Answer: It usually takes 4-6 weeks after approval, but timelines may vary depending on the state and verification process.

25. Question: How will I receive my Voter ID Card?
Answer: The EPIC will be delivered to your registered postal address free of cost through speed post under proper acknowledgment.

26. Question: What is an acknowledgment number?
Answer: After submitting Form 6, you receive an acknowledgment number which you can use to track the status of your application.

27. Question: How can I check the status of my Voter ID application?
Answer: You can check the status online on the NVSP portal (www.nvsp.in) using your acknowledgment number or reference ID.

28. Question: Can service personnel apply for Voter ID at their place of posting?
Answer: Yes. Service personnel can apply for enrollment as a general elector at their place of posting at a peace station, ensuring they are not already enrolled elsewhere.

29. Question: What should I do if I am already enrolled in another constituency?
Answer: You should not apply again. You can apply for transfer of your name from one constituency to another using Form 8.

30. Question: What if my name already exists in the electoral roll?
Answer: If your name is already enrolled, you should not apply using Form 6. You can use other forms for correction, deletion, or transfer.

31. Question: What is the qualifying date for voter enrollment?
Answer: The qualifying date is usually January 1st of each year. You must be 18 years or older on this date to be eligible.

32. Question: Can persons with disabilities apply for Voter ID?
Answer: Yes. Persons with disabilities can apply and indicate their category of disability (locomotive, visual, deaf & dumb, or other) and percentage in Form 6.

33. Question: What accessibility provisions are there for persons with intellectual disabilities?
Answer: In case of persons with intellectual disability, autism, cerebral palsy, or multiple disabilities, the signature or thumb impression of the person or their legal guardian is required.

34. Question: Should I attach disability certificate with Form 6?
Answer: Yes, if you are indicating disability, you should attach a disability certificate and mention the percentage.

35. Question: What if I don't have all the required documents?
Answer: If you don't have the mentioned documents, you can submit any other relevant document and mention it in the declaration. Field verification will be done.

36. Question: Can I mention family members already enrolled at my address?
Answer: Yes. You can mention the name, relationship, and EPIC number of a family member already enrolled at your current address.

37. Question: Do I need to visit the ERO office to submit Form 6?
Answer: You can submit Form 6 online through NVSP portal or offline by visiting the ERO office or through designated centers during special drives.

38. Question: What is the official website for Voter ID registration?
Answer: The official website is the National Voters' Service Portal at www.nvsp.in.

39. Question: Can I apply for Voter ID if I am living in a rented house?
Answer: Yes. You can use a registered rent/lease deed as proof of address or use your landlord's address proof along with a declaration.

40. Question: What should I write in the place of birth section?
Answer: You should mention the village/town, district, and state/UT where you were born.

41. Question: What does "ordinarily resident" mean?
Answer: Ordinarily resident means the place where you normally reside, not just a temporary stay. You must mention since when (month and year) you have been residing at that address.

42. Question: Can I apply for Voter ID if I am a Non-Resident Indian (NRI)?
Answer: Yes. NRIs can register as voters without having to be physically present in India, but separate provisions apply.

43. Question: What should I do after submitting Form 6?
Answer: After submission, you will receive an acknowledgment number. Use it to track your application status and wait for field verification if required.

44. Question: Will my application be verified?
Answer: Yes. The ERO or designated officer may conduct field verification to confirm your details, especially if standard documents are not provided.

45. Question: Can I apply for Voter ID for my family members together?
Answer: Yes. You can submit multiple applications together, but each family member must fill a separate Form 6.

46. Question: What if my application is rejected?
Answer: If rejected, you will be informed of the reason. You can reapply after correcting the errors or providing the required documents.

47. Question: Can I download Form 6?
Answer: Yes. Form 6 can be downloaded from the NVSP portal (www.nvsp.in) or your state's Chief Electoral Officer website.

48. Question: Is signature required on the photograph?
Answer: No. The photograph should be unsigned.

49. Question: What if I make a mistake in filling Form 6?
Answer: Ensure all entries are correct before submission. If submitted, you may need to apply for correction using Form 8 after enrollment.

50. Question: Can I apply for Voter ID during election period?
Answer: Application can be submitted anytime, but enrollment may be frozen during the election period due to the Model Code of Conduct. Processing will resume after elections.


==================================================
SECTION A: UNDERSTAND ELIGIBILITY AND REPORTING WINDOW
==================================================

1. Confirm that a death has occurred in India.
2. Note the exact date and time of death.
3. Note the exact place of death (hospital name and address, or home address).
4. Check if the death occurred in a hospital/nursing home (institutional death) or at home (domiciliary death).
5. Check if the death was due to natural causes or unnatural causes (accident, suicide, homicide).
6. Calculate how many days have passed since the death.
7. If within 21 days, proceed with normal registration (free).
8. If 22-30 days have passed, prepare for late registration with small fee.
9. If 31 days to 1 year has passed, prepare for late registration with affidavit and higher authority permission.
10. If more than 1 year has passed, understand that Magistrate's order is required.
11. Confirm you are applying in the jurisdiction where the death occurred.
12. If death was due to unnatural causes, confirm that police have been informed and FIR filed.

==================================================
SECTION B: GATHER REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==================================================

13. If institutional death, collect the Medical Certificate of Cause of Death (MCCD) from the hospital.
14. Ensure MCCD is signed and stamped by the attending doctor or Medical Officer.
15. MCCD mentions: Deceased's name, age, gender, date and time of death, cause of death.
16. If home death, request a death certificate from the attending doctor (if any).
17. If no doctor was present, note that post-mortem may be required (especially if cause is unclear).
18. Collect cremation receipt from the crematorium where the body was cremated.
19. Or collect burial receipt from the cemetery/graveyard where the body was buried.
20. Ensure cremation/burial receipt is original and mentions: Deceased's name, date of cremation/burial, name of crematorium/cemetery.
21. Collect deceased's identity proof documents (any available):
    - Aadhaar Card
    - Voter ID Card
    - Passport
    - Ration Card
    - PAN Card
    - Driving License
    - Any government-issued ID
22. Make photocopies of deceased's identity documents.
23. Collect identity proof of the informant (person reporting the death):
    - Aadhaar Card (preferred)
    - Voter ID
    - Passport
    - Any valid ID
24. Make photocopy of informant's identity proof.
25. Collect address proof of the informant.
26. If delayed registration (after 21 days), prepare a notarized affidavit stating the reason for delay.
27. If delayed by more than 1 year, prepare to apply for Non-Availability Certificate (NAC).
28. If delayed by more than 1 year, consult a lawyer or approach the local Magistrate's office.
29. If death was due to accident or unnatural cause, collect:
    - Copy of FIR (First Information Report)
    - Post-mortem report
    - Police panchnama (if available)
30. Keep all documents organized in a clean folder.

==================================================
SECTION C: IDENTIFY THE CORRECT REGISTRATION PORTAL
==================================================

31. Determine your state or union territory where death occurred.
32. Search online for "[Your State] death registration portal" or "CRS portal [Your State]".
33. Visit the official state government or municipal corporation website.
34. Common state portals:
    - Karnataka: eJanMa (ejanma.karnataka.gov.in) or Seva Sindhu
    - Delhi: MCD Online (mcdonline.nic.in)
    - Maharashtra: Aaple Sarkar (aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
    - Uttar Pradesh: e-Nagarsewa
    - Tamil Nadu: CRS TN (www.tnrd.gov.in)
    - Andhra Pradesh: MeeSeva
    - National: Civil Registration System (crsorgi.gov.in)
35. Bookmark the correct portal URL.
36. Check if your state offers online death registration and certificate download.
37. Note the portal's working hours and maintenance schedules if any.

==================================================
SECTION D: REGISTER ON THE PORTAL (IF APPLYING ONLINE)
==================================================

38. Open the official death registration portal.
39. Look for "Register" or "Sign Up" or "General Public Sign Up" button.
40. Click on it.
41. Enter your full name (informant/family member).
42. Enter your valid email address.
43. Enter your mobile number.
44. Enter a strong password.
45. Re-enter the password to confirm.
46. Enter captcha code if displayed.
47. Click "Register" or "Submit".
48. You will receive an OTP (One-Time Password) on your mobile and/or email.
49. Enter the OTP in the portal.
50. Click "Verify".
51. Your account is now created.
52. Note down your username and password securely.

==================================================
SECTION E: LOGIN TO THE PORTAL
==================================================

53. Go to the portal homepage.
54. Click on "Login" or "Sign In".
55. Enter your registered email or username.
56. Enter your password.
57. Enter captcha if required.
58. Click "Login".
59. You will be redirected to your dashboard.

==================================================
SECTION F: FILL DEATH REGISTRATION APPLICATION (FORM 2)
==================================================

60. On the dashboard, look for "Apply for Death Registration" or "New Death Registration" or "Form 2".
61. Click on it.
62. A new application form will open.
63. Read all instructions carefully on the form.
64. **Section 1: Event Details**
65. Select "Type of Death": Institutional (Hospital) or Domiciliary (Home).
66. Enter the exact date of death in DD/MM/YYYY format.
67. Enter time of death (if known).
68. Enter place of death: Hospital name and full address OR home full address.
69. Enter district, state, and PIN code of place of death.
70. **Section 2: Deceased Person's Details**
71. Enter deceased's full name (as per Aadhaar or available ID).
72. Select gender of the deceased: Male / Female / Third Gender.
73. Enter deceased's Aadhaar number (if available).
74. Enter deceased's date of birth (if known).
75. Enter deceased's age at the time of death (in years).
76. Enter deceased's father's name.
77. Enter deceased's mother's name.
78. If deceased was married, enter spouse's name.
79. Enter deceased's occupation.
80. Enter deceased's religion (optional).
81. Enter deceased's nationality (Indian).
82. **Section 3: Deceased's Permanent Address**
83. Enter complete residential address: House/Flat number, Street, Locality, Town/City, District, State, PIN code.
84. **Section 4: Cause of Death**
85. Select manner of death: Natural / Accidental / Suicide / Homicide / Unknown.
86. Enter cause of death as mentioned in MCCD or post-mortem report.
87. If medical attention was received before death, tick "Yes" and enter doctor's/hospital's name.
88. If no medical attention, tick "No".
89. **Section 5: Place of Disposal (Cremation/Burial)**
90. Enter name of crematorium or cemetery/graveyard.
91. Enter full address of crematorium/cemetery.
92. Enter date of cremation or burial.
93. **Section 6: Informant's Details** (Person reporting the death)
94. Enter informant's full name.
95. Enter informant's relationship with the deceased (Son / Daughter / Spouse / Head of Family / Hospital Authority / Other).
96. Enter informant's contact number.
97. Enter informant's email address (if available).
98. Enter informant's address.
99. **Section 7: Declaration**
100. Read the declaration statement carefully.
101. Tick the checkbox confirming that all details provided are true and correct.
102. Tick the checkbox acknowledging that providing false information is a punishable offense.

==================================================
SECTION G: UPLOAD REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==================================================

103. Look for "Upload Documents" or "Attach Documents" section on the form.
104. **If Institutional Death:**
105. Click "Choose File" for "Medical Certificate of Cause of Death (MCCD)".
106. Select the scanned PDF or image file of the MCCD issued by the hospital.
107. Ensure file is clear and readable.
108. Ensure file size is within limit (usually 200 KB to 2 MB).
109. Upload the file.
110. **For All Deaths:**
111. Click "Choose File" for "Cremation/Burial Receipt".
112. Select scanned copy of the cremation or burial receipt.
113. Ensure file is clear and legible.
114. Upload the file.
115. Click "Choose File" for "Deceased's Identity Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport / Any ID)".
116. Upload scanned copy of deceased's ID.
117. Click "Choose File" for "Informant's Identity Proof".
118. Upload scanned copy of informant's Aadhaar or ID.
119. **If Death Due to Unnatural Cause:**
120. Click "Choose File" for "Post-Mortem Report".
121. Upload the post-mortem report.
122. Click "Choose File" for "FIR Copy" (if applicable).
123. Upload copy of the FIR.
124. **If Delayed Registration:**
125. Click "Choose File" for "Affidavit for Delayed Registration".
126. Upload notarized affidavit stating reason for delay.
127. If more than 1 year delay, upload "Magistrate's Order" if already obtained.
128. **If Home Death:**
129. Click "Choose File" for "Doctor's Certificate" (if available).
130. Upload the doctor's certificate or declaration.
131. Review all uploaded documents on screen.
132. Ensure all uploads are complete and correct.

==================================================
SECTION H: REVIEW AND SUBMIT APPLICATION
==================================================

133. After filling all sections and uploading documents, click "Preview" or "Review Application".
134. The entire application will be displayed on screen.
135. Carefully check all entered details: deceased's details, cause of death, place of death, informant's details.
136. Verify uploaded documents are correct and clear.
137. If any error is found, click "Edit" or "Go Back" and correct the error.
138. If all details are correct, click "Submit" or "Final Submit".
139. A confirmation message will appear on screen.
140. An application reference number or acknowledgment number will be generated.
141. Note down the application reference number carefully.
142. Take a screenshot of the confirmation screen.
143. You may receive a confirmation SMS and email on your registered mobile and email.

==================================================
SECTION I: PAYMENT (IF APPLICABLE)
==================================================

144. If registration is within 21 days, usually no fee is required.
145. If delayed registration, a fee may be applicable.
146. The portal will display the fee amount (e.g., ₹10, ₹50, ₹100 depending on state and delay).
147. Click "Proceed to Pay" or "Make Payment".
148. Choose payment mode: Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.
149. Enter card details or select bank.
150. Complete the payment transaction.
151. Wait for payment confirmation.
152. Download payment receipt.
153. Save the payment receipt PDF.
154. Take a screenshot of payment confirmation.

==================================================
SECTION J: DOWNLOAD ACKNOWLEDGMENT RECEIPT
==================================================

155. After submission, look for "Download Acknowledgment" or "Print Application" button.
156. Click on it.
157. A PDF file of the acknowledgment will be generated.
158. Download the PDF to your computer or phone.
159. Open and verify the acknowledgment contains:
     - Application reference number
     - Date of submission
     - Deceased's details
     - Informant's details
160. Print the acknowledgment receipt.
161. Keep the printed acknowledgment safely.
162. Save the PDF in your email or cloud storage.

==================================================
SECTION K: TRACK APPLICATION STATUS
==================================================

163. Wait for processing by the local Registrar.
164. Processing usually takes 7 to 15 days for normal registration.
165. Delayed registrations may take longer (15 to 30 days).
166. To track status, visit the portal again.
167. Click "Track Application Status" or "Application Status" on homepage.
168. Enter your application reference number.
169. Enter other required details: deceased's date of death, name, etc.
170. Enter captcha if required.
171. Click "Submit" or "Check Status".
172. Status will be displayed: Pending / Under Verification / Approved / Rejected.
173. If status is "Approved", proceed to download certificate.
174. If status is "Rejected", note the reason for rejection.
175. If rejected, correct errors and reapply or contact Registrar's office.

==================================================
SECTION L: DOWNLOAD DEATH CERTIFICATE
==================================================

176. Once approved, visit the portal homepage.
177. Look for "Download Death Certificate" or "Search and Download Certificate" option.
178. Click on it.
179. You will be asked to enter search criteria.
180. **Option 1: Search by Registration Number**
181. If you have the registration number (from acknowledgment or hospital), enter it.
182. Enter deceased's date of death.
183. Enter captcha.
184. Click "Search" or "Submit".
185. **Option 2: Search by Details (if registration number not available)**
186. Enter deceased's date of death.
187. Enter deceased's name.
188. Enter deceased's gender.
189. Enter place of death.
190. Enter captcha.
191. Click "Search".
192. The death record will be displayed on screen.
193. Verify all details are correct: deceased's name, date of death, cause of death, place.
194. Click "Download Certificate" or "Get Certificate".
195. A fee may be required for downloading additional copies (usually ₹10 to ₹50).
196. If fee is required, proceed to payment.
197. Make payment using Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.
198. After successful payment, the certificate PDF will be generated.
199. Download the PDF file.
200. Save the PDF to your computer or phone.
201. Open the PDF and verify all details:
     - Deceased's name
     - Date of death
     - Place of death
     - Cause of death
     - Registration number
     - Digital signature and seal
202. Print the certificate on A4 size paper.
203. The printed certificate is legally valid for all purposes (insurance, bank, property, etc.).
204. Make multiple photocopies for records.

==================================================
SECTION M: OFFLINE APPLICATION (IF ONLINE NOT AVAILABLE)
==================================================

205. If online portal is not available or you prefer offline, visit the local Registrar's office.
206. Registrar's office is located at:
     - Municipal Corporation office (in urban areas)
     - Gram Panchayat office (in rural areas)
207. Note the office address and working hours (usually 10 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Friday).
208. Visit the office during working hours.
209. Look for "Birth and Death Registration" counter or section.
210. Collect Form 2 (Death Registration Form) from the counter.
211. Or download and print Form 2 from the portal before visiting.
212. Fill Form 2 completely and accurately using a blue or black pen.
213. Write clearly in capital letters.
214. Attach all required documents:
     - MCCD (if institutional death)
     - Cremation/burial receipt
     - Deceased's identity proof
     - Informant's identity proof
     - Affidavit (if delayed registration)
     - Post-mortem report and FIR (if unnatural death)
215. Submit the filled form with attachments at the counter.
216. Hand over original documents for verification.
217. Officer will verify all documents.
218. If documents are in order, officer will accept the form.
219. Officer will provide an acknowledgment receipt with a reference number.
220. Note the reference number carefully.
221. Keep the acknowledgment receipt safely.
222. If fee is applicable, pay at the cash counter and collect payment receipt.
223. Ask the officer about the expected timeline for certificate issuance.
224. The certificate will be issued within 7 to 15 days for normal registration.
225. You will be notified to collect the certificate from the office or it may be sent by post.
226. Visit the office on the specified date to collect the certificate.
227. Verify all details on the certificate before leaving the office.

==================================================
SECTION N: APPLY FOR DUPLICATE CERTIFICATE (IF LOST)
==================================================

228. If the original certificate is lost, apply for a duplicate.
229. Visit the portal and look for "Duplicate Death Certificate" or "Search and Download".
230. Enter registration number or search by deceased's details.
231. Pay the duplicate certificate fee (usually ₹10 to ₹50).
232. Download the duplicate certificate PDF.
233. Print and use as needed.


SECTION A: UNDERSTANDING LEARNER'S LICENCE BASICS
1. Understand that a learner's licence is a temporary permit.
2. Understand that it allows supervised driving practice.
3. Understand that it is mandatory before getting a permanent driving licence.
4. Know the vehicle class you want to apply for.
5. Know the minimum age requirement for your vehicle class.
6. Know the educational requirement if applying for commercial vehicle class.

SECTION B: CHECK ELIGIBILITY
7. Check minimum age: 16 years for motorcycles without gear (up to 50 cc).
8. Check minimum age: 18 years for motorcycles with gear.
9. Check minimum age: 18 years for light motor vehicles (cars).
10. Check minimum age: 18 years (or 20 years in some states) for commercial heavy vehicles.
11. If below 18 years, ensure parental or guardian consent is available.
12. If applying for commercial vehicle class, ensure 8th standard schooling is completed.
13. Ensure you understand basic traffic rules and signals.

SECTION C: GATHER REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
14. Collect proof of address documents.
15. Choose any one: electricity bill, telephone bill, LIC bond, Aadhaar card, passport, house agreement, or ration card.
16. Collect age proof documents.
17. Choose any one: PAN card, birth certificate, transfer certificate, 10th mark sheet, or passport.
18. Get at least six passport-size photographs.
19. If residing in another city, collect rental agreement and recent utility bill.
20. If applicable, get medical certificate Form 1A and Form 1 signed by a certified government doctor.
21. Download or obtain the learner's licence application form.
22. Keep scanned copies ready if applying online.
23. Keep originals ready if applying offline.

SECTION D: FILL APPLICATION FORM
24. Download the learner's licence application form from the state RTO website or Parivahan portal.
25. Or obtain the form from the nearest RTO office.
26. Fill Section 1: Particulars to be furnished by the applicant.
27. Enter full name.
28. Enter complete address.
29. Enter date of birth.
30. Enter place of birth.
31. Enter educational qualification.
32. Enter type of Indian citizenship.
33. Enter blood group.
34. Enter previous driving licence details if any.
35. Enter driving school certificate details if applicable.
36. Mention exemption from medical test if applicable.
37. Fill Section 2: Declaration by parent/guardian if applicant is below 18 years.
38. Ensure all details are accurate.
39. Attach passport-size photographs if required.
40. Sign the form.

SECTION E: ONLINE APPLICATION PROCESS
41. Visit your state RTO website or the Parivahan portal.
42. Check if online learner's licence application is available.
43. Click on "Online Services" or "Driving Licence Related Services".
44. Select "Apply for Learner's Licence".
45. Select your state if prompted.
46. Fill in required details: name, father's name, district, postal code, Aadhaar number, region.
47. Select the vehicle class.
48. Upload scanned proof of address.
49. Upload scanned age proof.
50. Upload passport-size photograph.
51. Upload medical certificate if applicable.
52. Choose test date and time if the portal allows.
53. Pay the application fee online (Rs. 150 + Rs. 50 test fee).
54. Complete payment through the online payment gateway.
55. Download the payment receipt.
56. Save the application reference number.
57. Download the application form if required by your RTO.
58. Print the receipt and application form.
59. Attend the RTO or test facility at the selected slot.

SECTION F: ONLINE APPLICATION THROUGH PARIVAHAN PORTAL
60. Visit the official Parivahan portal.
61. Click on "Online Services" tab at the top.
62. Select "Driving Licence Related Services" from the drop-down menu.
63. Choose your state.
64. On the redirected page, click on "Apply for Learner's Licence".
65. Fill in required applicant details.
66. Upload required documents.
67. Click on "Fee/Payments" tab.
68. Enter required payment details.
69. Click "Click here to calculate fees".
70. Choose payment gateway.
71. Complete the payment.
72. Download confirmation and receipt.
73. Save application reference number.
74. Print receipt.

SECTION G: OFFLINE APPLICATION PROCESS
75. Visit the nearest RTO office.
76. Collect Form 2 or Form 3 from the RTO counter.
77. Or download the form from the RTO website and print it.
78. Fill the form completely.
79. Attach passport-size photographs if required.
80. Attach proof of address document.
81. Attach age proof document.
82. Attach medical certificate if applicable.
83. Submit the completed form at the designated counter.
84. Pay the application fee (Rs. 200) at the RTO counter.
85. Collect the payment receipt.
86. Book a learner's licence test slot.
87. Note down the test date and time.
88. Collect acknowledgement or token number.
89. Keep all receipts and documents safe.

SECTION H: LEARNER'S LICENCE TEST PREPARATION
90. Study traffic rules and regulations.
91. Study road safety signals.
92. Study traffic signs.
93. Review sample learner's licence test questions if available online.
94. Understand multiple-choice question format.
95. Know the minimum passing score.
96. Prepare to answer questions on paper or online.

SECTION I: ON TEST DAY
97. Reach the test venue at least 30 minutes before the scheduled time.
98. Carry original documents.
99. Carry payment receipt.
100. Carry application form if required.
101. If applied online, carry the confirmation slip.
102. Submit confirmation slip at the appropriate counter if required.
103. Wait for your turn.
104. Enter the test room when called.
105. Read all test instructions carefully.
106. Answer all multiple-choice questions.
107. Review your answers before submitting.
108. Submit the test.
109. Wait for the result.

SECTION J: AFTER THE TEST
110. If you pass, collect your learner's licence.
111. Some RTOs issue the licence on the spot.
112. Some RTOs send the licence to your registered address.
113. Check all details on the learner's licence immediately.
114. Verify name, date of birth, photograph, and vehicle class.
115. Keep the learner's licence safely.
116. If you fail, ask about the retest process.
117. Pay the retest fee if applicable.
118. Book a new test slot after one week.
119. Prepare again and retake the test.

SECTION K: USING LEARNER'S LICENCE
120. Always carry your learner's licence while practicing driving.
121. Always be accompanied by a person holding a valid permanent driving licence for the same vehicle class.
122. Display an 'L' sign on your vehicle.
123. Follow all traffic rules.
124. Do not exceed speed limits applicable to learners.
125. Do not drive alone.
126. Practice regularly under supervision.
127. Familiarize yourself with road rules.
128. Prepare for the permanent driving licence test.

SECTION L: APPLYING FOR PERMANENT DRIVING LICENCE
129. Wait for at least 30 days after receiving your learner's licence.
130. Apply for a permanent driving licence after 30 days.
131. Book a driving test slot.
132. Practice driving skills thoroughly before the driving test.
133. Attend the driving test at the RTO.
134. Pass the driving test.
135. Collect your permanent driving licence.

SECTION M: VALIDITY AND RENEWAL
136. Remember that learner's licence is valid for six months only.
137. Apply for permanent driving licence before the learner's licence expires.
138. If learner's licence expires, you cannot renew it.
139. Apply for a fresh learner's licence if expired.

SECTION N: CHECKING APPLICATION STATUS
140. Visit the Parivahan Sewa website.
141. Or visit your state RTO website.
142. Click on "Check Application Status".
143. Enter your application reference number.
144. Enter required details.
145. Submit and check status.
146. Save or print status for reference.

SECTION O: IMPORTANT REMINDERS
147. Ensure all details are accurate before submission.
148. Keep original documents safe.
149. Keep copies of all receipts and acknowledgements.
150. Attend the test on time.
151. Do not miss the test appointment.
152. Practice driving only under proper supervision.
153. Always display 'L' sign while learning.
154. Follow all learner's licence rules and restrictions.

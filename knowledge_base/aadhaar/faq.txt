==================================================
GENERAL INFORMATION
==================================================

1. Question: What is Aadhaar?
Answer: Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identification number issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) to residents of India based on their biometric and demographic data.

2. Question: Is Aadhaar mandatory in India?
Answer: Aadhaar is not mandatory for all services, but it is required for certain government schemes, subsidies, and identity verification purposes.

3. Question: Who can apply for Aadhaar?
Answer: Any resident of India, including children and infants, can apply for Aadhaar regardless of age or gender.

4. Question: What is UIDAI?
Answer: UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India) is the government authority responsible for issuing and managing Aadhaar numbers.

5. Question: Is Aadhaar proof of citizenship?
Answer: No. Aadhaar is only proof of identity and residence, not proof of citizenship.

6. Question: Can NRIs apply for Aadhaar?
Answer: Yes, NRIs can apply if they have resided in India for more than 182 days in the last 12 months.

7. Question: What is the validity of Aadhaar?
Answer: Aadhaar is valid for a lifetime and does not expire.

8. Question: Is Aadhaar free of cost?
Answer: Yes, enrollment for Aadhaar is completely free.

9. Question: Can a person have more than one Aadhaar?
Answer: No. Each individual can have only one Aadhaar number.

10. Question: Is Aadhaar linked to PAN mandatory?
Answer: Yes, linking Aadhaar with PAN is mandatory for income tax filing as per government rules.

==================================================
ENROLLMENT PROCESS
==================================================

11. Question: How can I enroll for Aadhaar?
Answer: Visit an authorized Aadhaar Enrollment Center, fill out the form, and provide biometric and demographic details.

12. Question: What details are collected during enrollment?
Answer: Name, date of birth, gender, address, mobile number, along with fingerprints, iris scan, and photograph.

13. Question: Can I apply for Aadhaar online?
Answer: You can book an appointment online, but biometric data must be submitted physically at the center.

14. Question: How long does Aadhaar generation take?
Answer: It usually takes 7 to 90 days after successful enrollment.

15. Question: What is an EID number?
Answer: EID (Enrollment ID) is a temporary number given at the time of Aadhaar enrollment used to track application status.

==================================================
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
==================================================

16. Question: What documents are required for Aadhaar enrollment?
Answer: Proof of Identity (PoI), Proof of Address (PoA), and Proof of Date of Birth (DoB).

17. Question: What can be used as Proof of Identity?
Answer: Documents like PAN card, passport, voter ID, or driving license.

18. Question: What can be used as Proof of Address?
Answer: Utility bills, bank statements, passport, or ration card.

19. Question: What can be used as Proof of Date of Birth?
Answer: Birth certificate, SSLC certificate, passport, or PAN card.

20. Question: Is a mobile number mandatory?
Answer: No, but it is highly recommended for OTP-based services and updates.

==================================================
AADHAAR UPDATE & CORRECTIONS
==================================================

21. Question: Can I update my Aadhaar details?
Answer: Yes. You can update demographic and biometric details.

22. Question: What details can be updated?
Answer: Name, address, date of birth, gender, mobile number, and email ID.

23. Question: How can I update Aadhaar details?
Answer: Online (for address) or by visiting an Aadhaar Seva Kendra.

24. Question: Is there a fee for Aadhaar updates?
Answer: Yes, a nominal fee (around ₹50) is charged for updates.

25. Question: How many times can I change my name in Aadhaar?
Answer: Name can be updated only a limited number of times (usually twice).

26. Question: How many times can date of birth be updated?
Answer: Date of birth can generally be updated only once with valid proof.

27. Question: Can I correct spelling mistakes in Aadhaar?
Answer: Yes, minor corrections are allowed with supporting documents.

==================================================
AADHAAR DOWNLOAD & STATUS
==================================================

28. Question: How can I download Aadhaar?
Answer: Visit the UIDAI website and download e-Aadhaar using Aadhaar number or EID.

29. Question: What is e-Aadhaar?
Answer: e-Aadhaar is a digitally signed electronic version of Aadhaar.

30. Question: Is e-Aadhaar valid?
Answer: Yes, it is legally valid like the physical Aadhaar card.

31. Question: How can I check Aadhaar status?
Answer: Use the UIDAI website and enter your EID or Aadhaar number.

32. Question: What is masked Aadhaar?
Answer: Masked Aadhaar hides the first 8 digits and shows only the last 4 digits.

==================================================
LINKING & USAGE
==================================================

33. Question: Why is Aadhaar linked to bank accounts?
Answer: For direct benefit transfer (DBT) and subsidy schemes.

34. Question: Is Aadhaar required for SIM card?
Answer: It is commonly used for identity verification but not mandatory.

35. Question: Is Aadhaar required for passport?
Answer: Aadhaar can be used as identity proof but is not mandatory.

36. Question: Can Aadhaar be used as address proof?
Answer: Yes, it is widely accepted as address proof.

==================================================
SPECIAL CASES
==================================================

37. Question: Can children get Aadhaar?
Answer: Yes, children can get Aadhaar (Baal Aadhaar).

38. Question: Is biometric required for children?
Answer: No for children below 5 years; biometrics are updated later.

39. Question: What is Baal Aadhaar?
Answer: Aadhaar issued for children below 5 years, shown in blue color.

40. Question: Do children need to update Aadhaar?
Answer: Yes, biometric update is mandatory at age 5 and 15.

==================================================
LOST, DUPLICATE & SECURITY
==================================================

41. Question: What if I lose my Aadhaar card?
Answer: You can download e-Aadhaar or order a reprint online.

42. Question: Can I get a duplicate Aadhaar?
Answer: Yes, you can request a reprint for a small fee.

43. Question: Is Aadhaar data secure?
Answer: Yes, UIDAI uses strong encryption and security measures.

44. Question: Can someone misuse my Aadhaar?
Answer: Misuse is rare, but you should avoid sharing OTP and sensitive details.

45. Question: Can I lock my Aadhaar biometrics?
Answer: Yes, UIDAI allows biometric locking for security.

==================================================
GRIEVANCES & SUPPORT
==================================================

46. Question: What to do if Aadhaar update is rejected?
Answer: Reapply with correct documents or visit the enrollment center.

47. Question: How can I contact UIDAI?
Answer: Through toll-free number 1947 or UIDAI official website.

48. Question: Can I track update request status?
Answer: Yes, using the Update Request Number (URN).

49. Question: How long do updates take?
Answer: Usually 5 to 30 days.

50. Question: How many Aadhaar copies can I get?
Answer: Unlimited copies can be downloaded or printed anytime.

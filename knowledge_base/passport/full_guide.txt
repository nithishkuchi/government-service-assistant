

OVERVIEW:
This guide explains how to use the Passport Seva Online Portal for major passport services. It covers portal registration, login, online application submission, payment, appointment scheduling, rescheduling rules, and appointment-day requirements. It includes detailed guidance for ordinary passport, Tatkaal passport, and diplomatic/official passport services.

==================================================
SERVICE 1: FRESH / REISSUE ORDINARY PASSPORT
==================================================

INITIAL REGISTRATION & LOGIN

Step 1: Portal Registration
What to do:
- Open the Passport Seva Online Portal
- Click on "Register" button on the navigation menu
- Fill in all required details
- Click on "Sign Up"
- Confirm your registered email ID

Expected result:
Your account is created successfully.

Microinstructions:
- Open official Passport Seva portal.
- Click Register.
- Fill all registration fields.
- Select correct Passport Office / RPO.
- Click Sign Up.
- Confirm email.

Step 2: Portal Login
What to do:
- Login using Registered Login ID
- Enter Password

Important note:
Password must be reset every 3 months as it expires.

Expected result:
You enter the dashboard successfully.

Microinstructions:
- Enter Login ID.
- Enter Password.
- Login.
- Reset password every 3 months.
- Keep password secure.

--------------------------------------------------
PREREQUISITE: REGIONAL PASSPORT OFFICE (RPO) SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Why RPO Selection Matters:
- Select RPO based on your current residing location
- Selected RPO will reflect the nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) during appointment scheduling

If Changing RPO:
If you are reissuing passport from a different Regional Passport Office than where the present passport was issued:
- Create a new login ID on the Passport Seva Portal
- You may use the same email ID previously used
- Click on "Register"
- Complete registration process
- Select new "Passport Office"
- Choose any PSK/RPO as per convenience

Flexibility:
Applicants may apply for passport services at any PSK/RPO across India based on preference.

Microinstructions:
- Choose RPO based on present residence.
- Create new login if changing RPO.
- Use same email if needed.
- Select new Passport Office carefully.

--------------------------------------------------
ONLINE APPLICATION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Access Services
What to do:
- On the dashboard, click on 'Services' button on the Overview page or left menu
- Click on "Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue of Passport"

Expected result:
Passport application form opens.

Microinstructions:
- Login.
- Open Services.
- Click Fresh Passport/Re-issue.

Step 2: Complete Application Form (9 Steps)

Step 01: Passport Type
- Select type of ordinary passport required

Step 02: Applicant Details
- Enter full name
- Enter date of birth
- Enter gender
- Enter nationality and other personal information

Step 03: Family Details
- Enter father's details
- Enter mother's details
- Enter spouse details if applicable

Step 04: Address Details
- Enter current residential address
- Enter State/Union Territory
- Enter pin code

Step 05: Emergency Contact
- Enter emergency contact name
- Enter emergency contact details
- Enter relationship to applicant

Step 06: Previous Passport
- Enter previous passport details if applicable
- Enter passport number
- Enter issue date
- Enter expiry date

Step 07: Other Details
- Enter additional information as required
- Enter occupation details
- Enter marital status

Step 08: Passport Preview
- Review all entered information
- Verify accuracy

Step 09: Details Verification
- Perform final verification of all details

Important Note:
You can save the application at any point and resume later.

Common mistake:
Submitting without checking spelling, DOB, address, and passport category.

If stuck, do this:
Save the application and resume later after cross-checking documents.

Expected result:
Application is ready for final submission.

Microinstructions:
- Fill all 9 steps carefully.
- Match details with supporting documents.
- Review preview page.
- Verify every field.
- Save and continue later if needed.

Step 3: Submit Application
What to do:
- Carefully validate all details before submitting
- Click "Submit"

Important note:
You will NOT be able to make changes once application is submitted.

Expected result:
A Unique Application Reference Number (ARN) is generated.

Microinstructions:
- Recheck all fields.
- Click Submit.
- Note ARN immediately.
- Save ARN in phone and notebook.

--------------------------------------------------
PAYMENT & APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING
--------------------------------------------------

Step 4: Initiate Payment & Appointment
What to do:
- Click on "View > Pay and Schedule Appointment"

Important note:
Prior payment of applicable passport service fee is mandatory for booking appointments.

Step 5: Payment Process
What to do:
- Select "Online Payment"
- Select Appointment Quota: Normal
- Select preferred PSK or POPSK
- Review next available appointment date
- Click "Pay and Book Appointment"
- Complete payment at payment gateway

Expected result:
Payment is completed and appointment is booked.

Microinstructions:
- Open submitted application.
- Click Pay and Schedule Appointment.
- Choose Online Payment.
- Choose Normal quota.
- Select PSK/POPSK.
- Click Pay and Book Appointment.
- Complete payment.
- Save transaction details.

Step 6: Appointment Confirmation
What happens:
- Portal displays Appointment Confirmation screen
- SMS is sent to registered mobile number with appointment date, time, and PSK details

Expected result:
Appointment is confirmed.

Microinstructions:
- Check confirmation screen.
- Save appointment details.
- Save SMS.

Step 7: Appointment Rescheduling
What to do:
- Use "Schedule/Reschedule Appointment" link if needed

Rules:
- Normal users can reschedule appointment 3 times

Microinstructions:
- Use reschedule option only if needed.
- Track number of reschedules used.

--------------------------------------------------
ON THE DAY OF APPOINTMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Important reminders:
- Arrive on scheduled appointment date and time
- Carry all necessary original documents
- Carry photocopies if needed
- Service is provided at the discretion of Passport Seva Kendra In-charge / Passport Officer

Special note for minor applicants:
For applicants below 4 years of age:
- Carry recent passport size photograph (4.5 X 3.5 cm)
- Photograph must have white background

Important deadline:
Resubmission of application form is required if applicant does not visit PSK within 90 days from online form submission.

Microinstructions:
- Reach on time.
- Carry originals.
- Carry photocopies if needed.
- Carry child photo if applicant is below 4 years.
- Attend within 90 days.

SERVICE 2: TATKAAL PASSPORT
==================================================

WHAT IS TATKAAL?
Tatkaal Passport is an expedited passport processing service for urgent travel requirements.

REGISTRATION & LOGIN
Follow the same process as Fresh / Reissue Ordinary Passport:
- Register
- Confirm email
- Login with Login ID and Password

--------------------------------------------------
DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR TATKAAL
--------------------------------------------------

For Applicants Above 18 Years of Age:
- Submit address proof
- Submit birth proof
- Submit Non-ECR proof if applicable
- Submit ANY 3 documents from the List of Acceptable Documents

For Applicants Below 18 Years of Age:
- Submit address proof
- Submit birth proof
- Submit Non-ECR proof if applicable
- Submit ANY 2 documents from Serial Numbers 1 to 6 in the List of Acceptable Documents

List of Acceptable Documents:
1. PVC Aadhaar Card / Complete Original Aadhaar Letter / e-Aadhaar Card
   - Must have digital signature verified mark issued by UIDAI
   - Small cut-out Aadhaar cards printed by non-UIDAI entities are not accepted

2. PAN Card

3. Student Photo Identity Card
   - Issued by recognized educational institutions

4. Birth Certificate
   - Issued under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969

5. Ration Card
   - Latest or updated version

6. Last Passport Issued
   - In case of reissue only

7. Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC)

8. Driving Licence
   - Valid and within jurisdiction of State of submission

9. Bank Passbook / Kisan Passbook / Post Office Passbook
   - With applicant photo attested
   - With latest transaction updated

10. SC / ST / OBC Certificate

11. Arms License
   - Issued under the Arms Act, 1959

12. Pension Documents
   - Ex-servicemen’s Pension book
   - Pension payment order to retired government employees
   - Ex-servicemen’s widow/dependent certificates
   - Old age pension order

13. Service Photo Identity Card
   - Issued by State/Central Government
   - PSU
   - Local bodies
   - Public limited companies

--------------------------------------------------
INELIGIBLE CATEGORIES FOR TATKAAL SCHEME
--------------------------------------------------

Universal Ineligibility:
- Name change cases
- Address outside RPO jurisdiction where restricted
- Annexure C minor cases
- Government repatriation cases
- Deportation cases
- Travelled on Emergency Certificate
- Restricted Naga origin cases
- Restricted J&K origin cases
- Criminal court cases under section 6(2)(e) or 6(2)(f)
- Adverse police report
- Watch list cases
- Habitual passport losers
- Earlier travelled on forged/fake passports

Fresh Issue Cases Ineligible:
- Citizens by Registration/Naturalization
- Adopted children
- Children out of wedlock with single parent
- Children born out of surrogacy
- Single parent minors

Re-issue Cases Ineligible:
- Lost/stolen passports
- Short validity passport reissue
- Passport damaged beyond recognition
- Change in sex / appearance / signature
- DOB / POB correction
- Change in father/mother/spouse name

--------------------------------------------------
UNDERTAKING FOR TATKAAL SCHEME
--------------------------------------------------

Mandatory requirement:
Applicants under Tatkaal scheme must fill:
- Undertaking for Tatkaal Scheme (Hindi), OR
- Undertaking for Tatkaal Scheme (English)

--------------------------------------------------
TATKAAL APPOINTMENT DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------

Important rule:
Effective from 01 April 2023, Tatkaal appointment can be rescheduled only ONE time.

--------------------------------------------------
ONLINE APPLICATION PROCESS (SAME AS ORDINARY)
--------------------------------------------------

Follow the same 9-step process as ordinary passport, but select Tatkaal where applicable.

Microinstructions:
- Register and login.
- Select Tatkaal service.
- Fill 9 steps.
- Keep Tatkaal documents ready.
- Fill Undertaking for Tatkaal Scheme.
- Submit.
- Save ARN.

--------------------------------------------------

Rescheduling:

Microinstructions:
- Choose Tatkaal quota.

SERVICE 3: DIPLOMATIC / OFFICIAL PASSPORT
==================================================

WHO NEEDS DIPLOMATIC / OFFICIAL PASSPORT?
- Government officials traveling on official business
- Diplomats
- Persons authorized by Government for official purposes

REGISTRATION & LOGIN

Step 1: Portal Registration
- Click Register
- Fill details
- Sign Up
- Confirm email

Step 2: Portal Login
- Login using Login ID and Password

--------------------------------------------------
ONLINE APPLICATION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------

Step 3: Access Application
- Click "Apply for Diplomatic/Official Passport"

Step 4: Complete Application Form
Fill in all required details:
- Personal information
- Official designation
- Purpose of passport
- Government authorization details
- Address details
- Emergency contact
- Other required information

Step 5: Submit Application
- Review all details carefully
- Click Submit
- ARN will be generated

Important note:
No changes can be made after submission.

Microinstructions:
- Click Diplomatic/Official service.
- Fill official details carefully.
- Review.
- Submit.
- Save ARN.

--------------------------------------------------
PAYMENT & APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING
--------------------------------------------------

Step 6: Initiate Payment & Appointment
- Click "View > Pay and Schedule Appointment"
- Prior online payment is mandatory

Step 7: Payment Process
- Select Online Payment
- Select quota:
  - Normal
  - Tatkaal, only if applicable
- Select preferred PSK / POPSK / CPV Division location
- Review date
- Click Pay and Book Appointment
- Complete payment

Step 8: Appointment Confirmation
- Confirmation screen displayed
- SMS sent with date, time, and location

Step 9: Appointment Rescheduling
- Use reschedule link if needed
- Normal reschedule limit: 3 times
- If Tatkaal applies: 1 time

--------------------------------------------------
ON THE DAY OF APPOINTMENT – SUBMISSION LOCATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Location 1: For New Delhi Applicants
- Consular, Passport and Visa (CPV) Division
- Patiala House, New Delhi
- Bring printed copy of online application
- Bring original documents

Location 2: For Other State Applicants
- Passport Office attached to present address
- Bring printed application form
- Bring original documents

Before appointment:
- Print online application form
- Prepare original documents
- Refer to printed form instruction page for checklist and specific requirements

Microinstructions:
- Print form.
- Carry originals.
- Check correct submission location.
- Reach on time.
- Follow officer instructions.

==================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES FOR ALL SERVICES
==================================================

General Information:
- Services are provided at the discretion of Passport Seva Kendra In-charge / Passport Officer
- All information must be accurate and truthful
- No false documentation will be tolerated
- Provide supporting documents for all claims

Payment Information:
- Prior payment is mandatory for appointment booking
- Payment is through online payment gateway only
- Keep payment receipt safely

Application Timeline:
- Visit PSK within 90 days of online form submission
- If not visited within 90 days, application must be submitted again

Password Management:
- Reset password every 3 months
- Do not share password
- Use strong password

SMS Notifications:
- Keep mobile number updated
- Save appointment SMS

Document Verification:
- Original documents will be verified at PSK
- Photocopies may also be required
- Refer to confirmation SMS and printed form instructions

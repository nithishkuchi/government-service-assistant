

SECTION A: COMMON PORTAL REGISTRATION
1. Open the official Passport Seva Online Portal.
2. Click on the “Register” button.
3. Fill in all required registration details.
4. Select the correct Passport Office / RPO during registration.
5. Click on “Sign Up”.
6. Open your registered email inbox.
7. Confirm your registered email ID.
8. Return to the portal login page.
9. Enter your Login ID.
10. Enter your Password.
11. Login to the portal.
12. Reset password every 3 months if required.
13. Do not share your password with anyone.
14. Use a strong password.

SECTION B: RPO SELECTION
15. Select RPO based on your current residing location.
16. Understand that selected RPO affects the PSK shown during scheduling.
17. If changing to a different RPO, create a new login ID.
18. Use the same email ID if needed.
19. Register again for the new Passport Office.
20. Select the new Passport Office carefully.
21. Choose PSK/RPO as per convenience.

SECTION C: SERVICE 1 – FRESH / REISSUE ORDINARY PASSPORT
22. Login to the dashboard.
23. Click on “Services”.
24. Click on “Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue of Passport”.
25. Start Step 01: Select Passport Type.
26. Start Step 02: Enter Applicant Details.
27. Enter full name.
28. Enter date of birth.
29. Enter gender.
30. Enter nationality and personal details.
31. Start Step 03: Enter Family Details.
32. Enter father’s details.
33. Enter mother’s details.
34. Enter spouse details if applicable.
35. Start Step 04: Enter Address Details.
36. Enter current residential address.
37. Select State/Union Territory.
38. Enter pin code.
39. Start Step 05: Enter Emergency Contact.
40. Enter emergency contact name.
41. Enter emergency contact details.
42. Enter relationship with emergency contact.
43. Start Step 06: Enter Previous Passport details if applicable.
44. Enter previous passport number.
45. Enter passport issue date.
46. Enter passport expiry date.
47. Start Step 07: Enter Other Details.
48. Enter occupation details.
49. Enter marital status.
50. Enter other required information.
51. Start Step 08: Passport Preview.
52. Review all entered information.
53. Start Step 09: Details Verification.
54. Verify every detail carefully.
55. Save application if needed and continue later.
56. Click “Submit”.
57. Do not submit until all details are correct.
58. Note down the ARN generated after submission.

SECTION D: PAYMENT & APPOINTMENT – ORDINARY PASSPORT
59. Open submitted application view.
60. Click “View > Pay and Schedule Appointment”.
61. Select “Online Payment”.
62. Select appointment quota as “Normal”.
63. Select preferred PSK or POPSK.
64. Check next available appointment date.
65. Verify appointment details.
66. Click “Pay and Book Appointment”.
67. Complete payment on payment gateway.
68. Wait for redirection back to portal.
69. Check Appointment Confirmation screen.
70. Save appointment details.
71. Check SMS sent to registered mobile number.
72. Save date, time, and location details.
73. Use reschedule option if needed.
74. Reschedule only within allowed limit of 3 times.

SECTION E: APPOINTMENT DAY – ORDINARY PASSPORT
75. Reach PSK/POPSK on scheduled date.
76. Reach on time.
77. Carry all original documents.
78. Carry photocopies if required.
79. Carry appointment confirmation details.
80. Cooperate during document verification.
81. Follow PSK officer instructions.
82. If applicant is below 4 years, carry recent passport-size photo.
83. Ensure child photo is 4.5 x 3.5 cm with white background.
84. Attend appointment within 90 days from online submission.

SECTION F: SERVICE 2 – TATKAAL PASSPORT
85. Register and login using the same portal process.
86. Open relevant passport application service.
87. Select Passport Type as Tatkaal.
88. Enter all applicant details.
89. Enter family details.
90. Enter address details.
91. Enter emergency contact.
92. Enter previous passport details if applicable.
93. Enter other required details.
94. Preview all details carefully.
95. Verify all details.
96. Submit application.
97. Note ARN after submission.
98. Fill Undertaking for Tatkaal Scheme in Hindi or English.
99. Keep Tatkaal supporting documents ready.
100. For applicants above 18, keep address proof ready.
101. For applicants above 18, keep birth proof ready.
102. For applicants above 18, keep Non-ECR proof if applicable.
103. For applicants above 18, keep any 3 acceptable documents ready.
104. For applicants below 18, keep address proof ready.
105. For applicants below 18, keep birth proof ready.
106. For applicants below 18, keep Non-ECR proof if applicable.
107. For applicants below 18, keep any 2 documents from serial numbers 1 to 6 ready.
108. Ensure Aadhaar is acceptable if submitted.
109. Do not use non-UIDAI small cut-out Aadhaar cards.
110. Check Tatkaal eligibility before payment.
111. Do not apply under Tatkaal if your case is ineligible.
112. Review ineligibility categories carefully.

SECTION G: PAYMENT & APPOINTMENT – TATKAAL
113. Click “View > Pay and Schedule Appointment”.
114. Select “Online Payment”.
115. Select appointment quota as “Tatkaal”.
116. Select preferred PSK/POPSK.
117. Check next available appointment date.
118. Click “Pay and Book Appointment”.
119. Complete Tatkaal payment.
120. Wait for confirmation screen.
121. Save appointment details.
122. Save SMS details.
123. Reschedule only if necessary.
124. Remember Tatkaal can be rescheduled only once.

SECTION H: TATKAAL INELIGIBILITY CHECK
125. Do not use Tatkaal for name change cases.
126. Do not use Tatkaal if present address is outside RPO jurisdiction where restricted.
127. Do not use Tatkaal for Annexure C minor cases where restricted.
128. Do not use Tatkaal for government repatriation cases.
129. Do not use Tatkaal for deportation cases.
130. Do not use Tatkaal if applicant travelled on Emergency Certificate.
131. Do not use Tatkaal in restricted Naga origin cases.
132. Do not use Tatkaal in restricted J&K origin cases.
133. Do not use Tatkaal where section 6(2)(e) or 6(2)(f) applies.
134. Do not use Tatkaal if adverse police report exists.
135. Do not use Tatkaal for habitual passport loser category.
136. Do not use Tatkaal for fake/forged passport history.
137. Do not use Tatkaal for citizenship by registration/naturalization fresh cases where restricted.
138. Do not use Tatkaal for adopted children where restricted.
139. Do not use Tatkaal for children out of wedlock with single parent where restricted.
140. Do not use Tatkaal for surrogacy children where restricted.
141. Do not use Tatkaal for single parent minors where restricted.
142. Do not use Tatkaal for lost/stolen passport reissue.
143. Do not use Tatkaal for short validity reissue.
144. Do not use Tatkaal for passport damaged beyond recognition.
145. Do not use Tatkaal for change in sex/appearance/signature.
146. Do not use Tatkaal for DOB/POB correction.
147. Do not use Tatkaal for change in father/mother/spouse name.

SECTION I: SERVICE 3 – DIPLOMATIC / OFFICIAL PASSPORT
148. Register on the portal.
149. Confirm email ID.
150. Login with Login ID and Password.
151. Click “Apply for Diplomatic/Official Passport”.
152. Enter personal information.
153. Enter official designation.
154. Enter purpose of passport.
155. Enter government authorization details.
156. Enter address details.
157. Enter emergency contact details.
158. Enter other required information.
159. Review all details carefully.
160. Click “Submit”.
161. Note ARN generated.

SECTION J: PAYMENT & APPOINTMENT – DIPLOMATIC / OFFICIAL
162. Click “View > Pay and Schedule Appointment”.
163. Select “Online Payment”.
164. Select appointment quota as applicable.
165. Select preferred PSK/POPSK or CPV Division location.
166. Check next available appointment date.
167. Click “Pay and Book Appointment”.
168. Complete payment on payment gateway.
169. Wait for confirmation screen.
170. Save appointment details.
171. Save SMS details.
172. Use reschedule option if needed.
173. Reschedule within allowed limit of 3 times.
174. If Tatkaal applies, reschedule only once.

SECTION K: APPOINTMENT DAY – DIPLOMATIC / OFFICIAL
175. Print the online filled application form.
176. Carry printed application form.
177. Carry original documents.
178. Refer to printed application instruction page.
179. For New Delhi applicants, go to CPV Division, Patiala House, New Delhi.
180. For other state applicants, go to Passport Office attached to present address.
181. Reach on time.
182. Follow officer instructions.

SECTION L: COMMON IMPORTANT ACTIONS
183. Ensure all details are truthful.
184. Do not submit false documents.
185. Keep payment receipt safely.
186. Attend appointment within 90 days from online submission.
187. If 90 days pass without visit, resubmit application.
188. Keep mobile number updated on portal.
189. Save all SMS notifications.
190. Keep ARN safely.
191. Keep appointment confirmation safely.
192. Carry originals for verification.
193. Carry photocopies if required.
194. Follow PSK/Passport Officer instructions.

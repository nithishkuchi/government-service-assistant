
==================================================
SECTION A: PREREQUISITE - OBTAIN LEARNER'S LICENCE FIRST
==================================================

1. Understand you CANNOT apply for Driving Licence without Learner's Licence.
2. Apply for Learner's Licence first for your desired vehicle class.
3. Visit state RTO website or Parivahan portal.
4. Download Form 2 or Form 3 for Learner's Licence application.
5. Fill all personal details in the form.
6. Attach passport-size photographs.
7. Attach proof of address document.
8. Attach proof of age document.
9. Submit form at RTO or online.
10. Pay Rs. 200 (Rs. 150 + Rs. 50 test fee).
11. Book slot for Learner's Licence test.
12. Study traffic rules and road signs.
13. Appear for Learner's Licence theory test.
14. Pass the test (minimum 60% marks usually required).
15. Collect your Learner's Licence on the spot or wait for postal delivery.
16. Note the issue date on your Learner's Licence.
17. Understand that Learner's Licence is valid for 6 months only.
18. Calculate the date 30 days after LL issue date.
19. Mark this date - you can apply for DL only after this date.
20. During these 30 days, practice driving under supervision.
21. Always be accompanied by a licensed driver while practicing.
22. Display 'L' sign on your vehicle during practice.
23. Do not drive alone with only Learner's Licence.
24. Follow all traffic rules during practice period.

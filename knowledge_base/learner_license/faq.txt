
1. Question: What is a Learner's Licence in India?
Answer: A learner's licence in India is a temporary driving permit that allows you to practice driving under supervision before obtaining a permanent driving licence.

2. Question: Who can apply for a Learner's Licence?
Answer: Any person who has reached the legal minimum age for the respective vehicle class can apply for a learner's licence.

3. Question: Is a Learner's Licence mandatory before getting a permanent driving licence?
Answer: Yes. You must first obtain a learner's licence for the relevant class of vehicle before applying for a permanent driving licence.

4. Question: What are the main types of Learner's Licence in India?
Answer: Learner's licences are issued based on vehicle class, such as MC 50CC, LMV-NT, FVG, MC EX50CC, MCWG, HGMV, LMV-TR, HPMV, and MGV.

5. Question: What does MC 50CC mean?
Answer: MC 50CC refers to motorcycles with engine capacity of 50 cc or less.

6. Question: What does LMV-NT mean?
Answer: LMV-NT means Light Motor Vehicle used for non-transport (personal) purposes, such as cars.

7. Question: What does FVG mean?
Answer: FVG refers to motorcycles of any engine capacity but without gears, such as scooters and mopeds.

8. Question: What does MC EX50CC mean?
Answer: MC EX50CC refers to motorcycles with engine capacity of 50 cc or more, including motorcycles with gear and light motor vehicles.

9. Question: What does MCWG mean?
Answer: MCWG or M/CYCL.WG refers to all types of motorcycles including motorcycles with gear.

10. Question: What does HGMV mean?
Answer: HGMV means Heavy Goods Motor Vehicle, used for commercial transport of heavy goods.

11. Question: What does LMV-TR mean?
Answer: LMV-TR means Light Motor Vehicle used for commercial (transport) purposes.

12. Question: What does HPMV mean?
Answer: HPMV means Heavy Passenger Motor Vehicle, used for carrying passengers commercially.

13. Question: What is the minimum age to apply for a Learner's Licence for motorcycles with gear?
Answer: The applicant must be at least 18 years old.

14. Question: What is the minimum age to apply for a Learner's Licence for motorcycles without gear with up to 50 cc capacity?
Answer: The applicant must be at least 16 years old. Parental or guardian consent is required if the applicant is below 18 years.

15. Question: Can a 17-year-old apply for a Learner's Licence in India?
Answer: Yes. A 17-year-old can apply for certain vehicle classes that allow the minimum age of 16 years, such as motorcycles without gear (up to 50 cc capacity).

16. Question: What is the minimum age for applying for a Learner's Licence for commercial heavy vehicles?
Answer: The applicant must be at least 18 years old. In some states, the minimum age requirement is 20 years.

17. Question: What are the educational qualification requirements for commercial vehicle learner's licence?
Answer: The applicant must have completed 8th standard of schooling.

18. Question: What details are present on a Learner's Licence?
Answer: The learner's licence contains the name and age of the holder, photograph, issuing authority, and a label specifying it is a learner's licence.

19. Question: What is the application fee for a Learner's Licence?
Answer: The application fee is Rs. 150, plus an additional Rs. 50 as a test fee, totaling Rs. 200.

20. Question: What documents are required for a Learner's Licence application?
Answer: You need proof of address, age proof, passport-size photographs, filled application form, and if applicable, a medical certificate.

21. Question: Which documents can be used as proof of address?
Answer: Electricity bill, telephone bill, LIC bond, Aadhaar card, passport, house agreement, or ration card.

22. Question: Which documents can be used as age proof?
Answer: PAN card, birth certificate, transfer certificate, 10th class mark sheet, or passport.

23. Question: How many passport-size photographs are required?
Answer: At least six copies of passport-size photographs are required.

24. Question: Is a medical certificate required for all Learner's Licence applicants?
Answer: A medical certificate (Form 1A and Form 1) signed by a certified government doctor is required if applicable, depending on vehicle class and age.

25. Question: Can I apply for a Learner's Licence online?
Answer: Yes. Many state RTO websites and the Parivahan portal offer online application for learner's licence.

26. Question: How do I apply for a Learner's Licence online?
Answer: Visit your state RTO website or the Parivahan portal, fill in required details, upload documents, pay the fee, and book a test slot.

27. Question: Can I apply for a Learner's Licence offline?
Answer: Yes. You can visit your nearest RTO office, fill Form 2 or Form 3, submit required documents, pay the fee, and book a test slot.

28. Question: What is Form 2 for a Learner's Licence?
Answer: Form 2 is the application form for learner's licence with passport photos, used during offline application.

29. Question: What is Form 3 for a Learner's Licence?
Answer: Form 3 is another application form for learner's licence with photographs, used during offline application.

30. Question: What is the Learner's Licence test procedure?
Answer: You must take a written or online multiple-choice test that assesses your knowledge of traffic rules and safety signals.

31. Question: Do I need to drive a vehicle to get a Learner's Licence?
Answer: No. You are not required to drive a vehicle during the learner's licence test. The test assesses your theoretical knowledge only.

32. Question: What happens if I fail the Learner's Licence test?
Answer: If you fail, you can retake the test after one week by paying the retest fee.

33. Question: How long is the Learner's Licence valid?
Answer: A learner's licence is valid for six months from the date of issue.

34. Question: Is my Learner's Licence valid throughout India?
Answer: Yes. Your learner's licence is valid throughout India for six months.

35. Question: Can I renew my Learner's Licence after it expires?
Answer: No. An expired learner's licence cannot be renewed. You must apply for a fresh learner's licence.

36. Question: What rules must be followed while driving with a Learner's Licence?
Answer: You must be accompanied by a person holding a valid permanent driving licence, display an 'L' sign on your vehicle, and follow speed restrictions.

37. Question: Is it mandatory to display an 'L' sign on the vehicle if I have a Learner's Licence?
Answer: Yes. Displaying an 'L' sign is compulsory to indicate that you are learning to drive.

38. Question: Can I drive alone with a Learner's Licence?
Answer: No. You must always be accompanied by someone who holds a valid permanent driving licence for that vehicle class.

39. Question: Can an illiterate person apply for a Learner's Licence?
Answer: Yes. Illiterate individuals can apply for a learner's licence.

40. Question: Can I apply for a Learner's Licence for both a non-gear vehicle and a car together?
Answer: Yes. You can apply for both in one application and take a combined test, but separate application fees apply for each vehicle class.

41. Question: When can I apply for a permanent driving licence after getting a Learner's Licence?
Answer: You can apply for a permanent driving licence 30 days after the issuance of your learner's licence.

42. Question: Can I upgrade my Learner's Licence to a permanent driving licence?
Answer: Yes. After holding a learner's licence for the required duration (usually 30 days) and passing the driving test, you can get a permanent driving licence.

43. Question: What happens if I do not attend the Learner's Licence test?
Answer: You will have to book a fresh appointment and pay the retest fee.

44. Question: How can I check the status of my Learner's Licence application?
Answer: You can check the status on the official Parivahan Sewa website or your state RTO website.

45. Question: Is a Learner's Licence valid for commercial vehicles?
Answer: A learner's licence is issued based on vehicle class. If you apply for a commercial vehicle class learner's licence, it will be valid for that class.

46. Question: Why do I need a Learner's Licence?
Answer: A learner's licence legally allows you to practice driving under supervision, helps you learn traffic rules, and is a prerequisite for obtaining a permanent driving licence.

47. Question: Can I practice driving without a Learner's Licence?
Answer: No. Driving without a valid learner's licence or permanent driving licence is illegal.

48. Question: Is a Learner's Licence required for insurance purposes?
Answer: Yes. Most vehicle insurance policies require the driver to possess a valid driving licence, which includes a learner's licence during the learning phase.

49. Question: What should I do if I lose my Learner's Licence?
Answer: Contact your RTO for the duplicate learner's licence issuance process and pay the applicable fee.

50. Question: Can I book my Learner's Licence test slot online?
Answer: Yes. Many state RTOs and the Parivahan portal allow online booking of learner's licence test slots.

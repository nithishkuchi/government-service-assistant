
==================================================
SECTION A: PRELIMINARY UNDERSTANDING AND RESEARCH
==================================================

1. Understand what a Caste Certificate is and its purpose.
2. Determine your caste category: SC / ST / OBC.
3. If OBC, determine if you fall under Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) or Creamy Layer.
4. Confirm your family annual income if applying for OBC-NCL.
5. Check if your caste is listed in the official Central or State SC/ST/OBC list.
6. Visit the National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC) website: ncsc.nic.in
7. Visit the National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST) website: ncst.nic.in
8. Visit the National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC) website: ncbc.nic.in
9. Verify your caste is in the official list for your state.
10. Identify your state or union territory.
11. Search online for "[Your State] caste certificate application" or "e-District [Your State]".
12. Visit your state's official e-District portal.
13. Common state portals:
    - Uttar Pradesh: edistrict.up.gov.in
    - West Bengal: wb.gov.in (Caste Certificate section)
    - Maharashtra: aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
    - Gujarat: digitalgujarat.gov.in
    - Bihar: serviceonline.bihar.gov.in
    - Delhi: edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in
    - Haryana: saralharyana.gov.in
    - Telangana: meeseva.telangana.gov.in
    - Odisha: odisha.gov.in
    - Punjab: punjab.gov.in
14. Bookmark the official portal URL.
15. Check if your state offers online caste certificate application.
16. Note the helpline numbers and office addresses.

==================================================
SECTION B: GATHER REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==================================================

17. Collect your Aadhaar Card (mandatory for most states).
18. Make a clear photocopy of Aadhaar Card (front and back).
19. Scan Aadhaar Card in PDF or JPEG format.
20. Ensure scan is clear, under 200 KB to 2 MB.
21. Collect Address Proof documents.
22. Choose one: Ration Card, Voter ID, Electricity bill, Water bill, Property tax receipt, Rent agreement.
23. Ensure address proof is recent (within last 6 months if utility bill).
24. Make a photocopy of address proof.
25. Scan address proof clearly.
26. **Collect Caste Proof / Lineage Documents:**
27. If father has a caste certificate, collect it (original and photocopy).
28. If grandfather has a caste certificate, collect it.
29. If you have your own school leaving certificate showing caste, collect it.
30. If you have Transfer Certificate (TC) showing caste, collect it.
31. If any sibling has a caste certificate, collect it.
32. Make photocopies of all caste proof documents.
33. Scan all caste proof documents.
34. **If applying for OBC-NCL:**
35. Collect Income Certificate (issued by Tehsildar).
36. Or collect Form 16 (if salaried).
37. Or collect Income Tax Return (ITR) of last financial year.
38. Or collect salary slips of last 6 months.
39. Ensure total family income shown is below ₹8 lakh per annum.
40. Make photocopies of income proof.
41. Scan income proof documents.
42. Collect passport-size photographs (3-4 copies).
43. Scan one passport-size photograph (JPEG, under 50 KB).
44. **Prepare Self-Declaration Affidavit:**
45. Buy stamp paper (₹10 to ₹20 depending on state).
46. Draft an affidavit stating:
    - Your name, father's name, date of birth
    - Your caste and sub-caste
    - Your permanent address
    - Declaration that you belong to the claimed caste
    - Declaration that information is true
47. Get the affidavit notarized or signed before a magistrate.
48. Make a photocopy of the affidavit.
49. Scan the notarized affidavit.
50. **Additional Documents (if required by state):**
51. Collect details of two local referees (name, address, contact number).
52. Collect their identity proof if required.
53. Keep all documents organized in a folder (physical and digital).

SECTION C: REGISTER ON E-DISTRICT PORTAL (ONLINE APPLICATION)
==================================================

54. Open a web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended).
55. Visit your state's e-District portal.
56. On the homepage, look for "New User Registration" or "Citizen Registration".
57. Click on it.
58. A registration form will open.
59. Enter your full name (as per Aadhaar).
60. Enter your father's name.
61. Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
62. Select your gender.
63. Enter your mobile number (active and accessible).
64. Enter your email address.
65. Enter your Aadhaar number.
66. Enter your complete address.
67. Select your district.
68. Select your Tehsil/Block.
69. Create a unique username.
70. Create a strong password.
71. Re-enter the password to confirm.
72. Enter the captcha code displayed.
73. Click "Register" or "Submit".
74. An OTP will be sent to your mobile number.
75. Enter the OTP in the portal.
76. Click "Verify".
77. Registration is complete.
78. Note down your username and password securely.

==================================================
SECTION D: LOGIN TO E-DISTRICT PORTAL
==================================================

79. Go to the e-District portal homepage.
80. Click "Citizen Login" or "Login".
81. Enter your registered username or email.
82. Enter your password.
83. Enter captcha if displayed.
84. Click "Login".
85. You will be redirected to your dashboard.

==================================================
SECTION E: FILL CASTE CERTIFICATE APPLICATION FORM
==================================================

86. On the dashboard, look for "Apply for Services" or "New Application".
87. Click on it.
88. A list of available services will be displayed.
89. Look for "Caste Certificate" or "SC/ST/OBC Certificate".
90. Click on it.
91. Select the category: SC / ST / OBC-NCL / OBC.
92. A new application form will open.
93. Read all instructions carefully.
94. **Section 1: Applicant Details**
95. Enter your full name (as per Aadhaar).
96. Enter your father's full name.
97. Enter your mother's full name.
98. Enter your grandfather's name (paternal).
99. Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
100. Select your gender.
101. Select your marital status.
102. Enter your Aadhaar number.
103. Enter your mobile number.
104. Enter your email address.
105. **Section 2: Address Details**
106. Enter your complete permanent address:
     - House/Flat number
     - Street/Road
     - Village/Town
     - Post Office
     - Tehsil/Taluka
     - District
     - State
     - PIN Code
107. If current address is different, enter current address.
108. **Section 3: Caste Details**
109. Select your caste category: SC / ST / OBC.
110. Select your sub-caste from the dropdown list.
111. If sub-caste not in list, type it manually.
112. Enter the name of your community/sub-caste.
113. **Section 4: Lineage/Paternal Relative Details**
114. Select the relative whose caste certificate you are using: Father / Grandfather / Brother / Uncle.
115. Enter the name of that relative.
116. Enter their caste certificate number (if available).
117. Enter the issuing authority of their certificate.
118. Enter the date of issue of their certificate.
119. **Section 5: Income Details (for OBC-NCL only)**
120. Enter your father's occupation.
121. Enter your father's annual income.
122. Enter your mother's occupation (if earning).
123. Enter your mother's annual income.
124. Enter details of any other earning family member.
125. Enter total family annual income.
126. Ensure total is below ₹8 lakh.
127. **Section 6: Referee Details (if required by state)**
128. Enter name of Referee 1.
129. Enter address of Referee 1.
130. Enter contact number of Referee 1.
131. Enter name of Referee 2.
132. Enter address of Referee 2.
133. Enter contact number of Referee 2.
134. **Section 7: Declaration**
135. Read the declaration statement carefully.
136. Tick the checkbox confirming:
     - All information provided is true and correct
     - You belong to the caste claimed
     - You understand false declaration is punishable
137. Tick acknowledgment of penalty for false information.

==================================================

138. Look for "Upload Documents" section.
139. **Upload Aadhaar Card:**
140. Click "Choose File" for Aadhaar Card.
141. Select the scanned Aadhaar PDF or JPEG.
142. Ensure file size is within limit (usually 200 KB to 2 MB).
143. Click "Upload".
144. **Upload Address Proof:**
145. Click "Choose File" for Address Proof.
146. Select scanned ration card, voter ID, or utility bill.
147. Upload the file.
148. **Upload Caste Proof / Lineage Document:**
149. Click "Choose File" for Father's/Grandfather's Caste Certificate.
150. Select the scanned caste certificate.
151. Upload the file.
152. If using school certificate, upload scanned school leaving certificate showing caste.
153. **Upload Income Proof (for OBC-NCL):**
154. Click "Choose File" for Income Certificate or Form 16.
155. Select scanned income proof.
156. Upload the file.
157. **Upload Self-Declaration Affidavit:**
158. Click "Choose File" for Affidavit.
159. Select the scanned notarized affidavit.
160. Upload the file.
161. **Upload Passport-Size Photograph:**
162. Click "Choose File" for Photograph.
163. Select scanned photograph (JPEG, under 50 KB).
164. Upload the file.
165. **Upload Additional Documents (if applicable):**
166. If PAN Card available, upload it.
167. If voter ID available, upload it.
168. Review all uploaded documents on screen.
169. Ensure all files are clear and correctly uploaded.

SECTION G: REVIEW AND SUBMIT APPLICATION
==================================================

170. After filling all sections and uploading documents, click "Preview" or "Review".
171. The entire application will be displayed.
172. Carefully verify:
     - Name, father's name, date of birth
     - Address
     - Caste and sub-caste
     - Lineage details
     - Uploaded documents
173. If any error, click "Edit" and correct.
174. If all correct, click "Submit" or "Final Submit".
175. A confirmation popup may appear.
176. Click "Yes" or "Confirm".
177. Application is submitted.
178. An Application Reference Number or Acknowledgment Number is generated.
179. Note the acknowledgment number carefully.
180. Take a screenshot.
181. You may receive SMS and email confirmation.

189. After submission, click "Download Acknowledgment" or "Print Application".

SECTION J: TRACK APPLICATION STATUS
==================================================

196. Wait for processing (usually 15-30 days).
197. To track status, visit the e-District portal.
198. Click "Track Application" or "Application Status".
199. Enter your acknowledgment number.
200. Enter other required details (DOB, mobile number).
201. Enter captcha.
202. Click "Submit" or "Track".
203. Status will be displayed:
     - Submitted / Pending
     - Under Verification / Field Inquiry Pending
     - Approved
     - Rejected
     - Certificate Ready for Download
204. If "Under Verification", wait for field visit.
205. If "Approved", proceed to download certificate.
206. If "Rejected", check reason and re-apply with corrections.

==================================================
SECTION K: FIELD VERIFICATION
==================================================

207. A Patwari, Lekhpal, or Village Revenue Officer may visit your home.
208. You will be informed via SMS or call.
209. Be present at home during the scheduled visit.
210. Keep all original documents ready:
     - Aadhaar Card
     - Address proof
     - Father's caste certificate
     - School certificates
     - Income proof (if OBC-NCL)
211. The officer will verify your identity and residence.
212. The officer may ask questions about your family and caste.
213. Answer honestly.
214. The officer may take photographs or notes.
215. Sign the verification report if asked.
216. Thank the officer.
217. Verification is usually completed within a day.
218. The officer submits the report to the Tehsildar.

==================================================
SECTION L: DOWNLOAD CASTE CERTIFICATE
==================================================

219. Once status shows "Approved" or "Certificate Ready", visit the portal.
220. Click "Download Certificate" or "View Certificate".
221. Enter acknowledgment number.
222. Enter verification details (DOB, etc.).
223. Enter captcha.
224. Click "Submit".
225. The Caste Certificate PDF will be generated.
226. Download the PDF.
227. Open and verify all details:
     - Your name
     - Father's name
     - Caste and sub-caste
     - Certificate number
     - Date of issue
     - Validity (if applicable)
     - Digital signature and seal of Tehsildar/SDM
228. Print the certificate on A4 paper.
229. The printed certificate is legally valid.
230. Make multiple photocopies.
231. Keep original safely.
232. Upload to DigiLocker for digital access.

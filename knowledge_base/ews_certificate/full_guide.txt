

An **Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Certificate**, officially known as an **"Income and Asset Certificate"**, is a government-issued document that allows individuals from the General (unreserved) category to avail 10% reservation in government jobs and educational institutions. It was introduced via the **103rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019**, to provide reservation benefits to economically disadvantaged individuals who are not covered under the existing SC, ST, or OBC reservation quotas.

**Issuing Authority:**
- Tehsildar
- Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)
- Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO)
- District Magistrate (DM)
- Collector
- Equivalent Revenue Officer (rank not below Tehsildar)

**Governing Legislation:**
- 103rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019
- Office Memoranda issued by the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Government of India
- State-specific revenue department guidelines

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR EWS CERTIFICATE
==================================================

To qualify for an EWS Certificate, an applicant must meet ALL of the following criteria (as per Central Government guidelines; states may have minor variations):

### 1. Category Criteria

- **Must belong to the General (unreserved) category.**
- **Must NOT be covered under any existing reservation quotas for SC, ST, or OBC.**

### 2. Family Income Criteria

- **Gross annual family income must be below ₹8 lakh.**
- This income limit applies to the financial year preceding the year of application.
- **Family income includes income from all sources:**
  - Salary
  - Agricultural income
  - Business income
  - Professional income
  - Rent
  - Interest
  - Dividends
  - Pension
  - Other sources
- **Definition of "Family" for Income:**
  - The applicant
  - Parents
  - Spouse
  - Siblings below the age of 18 years
  - Children below the age of 18 years
- **Income of married siblings and adult children is NOT included.**

### 3. Family Asset Criteria

The family must **NOT** own any of the following assets:

| Asset Type | Limit |
|------------|-------|
| **Agricultural Land** | Less than 5 acres |
| **Residential Flat** | Less than 1,000 sq. ft. |
| **Residential Plot (in Notified Municipalities)** | Less than 100 sq. yards |
| **Residential Plot (in Non-Notified Areas)** | Less than 200 sq. yards |

- **Definition of "Family" for Assets:**
  - Same as for income: applicant, parents, spouse, minor siblings, minor children.
- **Notified Municipalities:** Urban areas officially recognized by the government.
- **Non-Notified Areas:** Rural and semi-urban areas.
- **Asset Ownership:** All assets owned by the family in different locations must be clubbed together.

**Note:** If any of the income or asset criteria are not met, the applicant is NOT eligible for EWS Certificate.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==================================================

### Mandatory Documents

**1. Identity Proof (Any ONE)**
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory in most states)
- Voter ID Card
- PAN Card
- Passport
- Driving License

**2. Address Proof / Domicile Certificate (Any ONE)**
- Domicile Certificate issued by the state
- Aadhaar Card
- Voter ID Card
- Ration Card
- Electricity bill (within last 6 months)
- Water bill (within last 6 months)
- Rent agreement (registered)

**3. Income Proof (One or More, Depending on Income Source)**
- **Income Certificate** issued by Tehsildar (highly recommended)
- **Salary slips** (last 6 months)
- **Form 16** (for salaried employees)
- **Income Tax Return (ITR)** of last financial year
- **Bank statements** (last 6 months) showing income deposits
- **Notarized Income Affidavit** (if other proofs are not available)

**4. Asset Proof**
- **Land Records:** 7/12 extract, revenue records, land ownership documents (if agricultural land is owned)
- **Property Documents:** Sale deed, property tax receipts, municipal records (if residential property is owned)
- **Affidavit** declaring no ownership of assets above limits (if no property is owned)

**5. Self-Declaration Affidavit**
- Notarized affidavit on stamp paper (₹10 to ₹20)
- **Format:** Usually Annexure-B of DoPT guidelines or state-specific format
- Declares your category, income, and assets
- Acknowledges that false information is punishable

**6. Passport-Size Photograph**
- Recent passport-size color photograph (3-4 copies)
- For online: scanned photo (JPEG, under 50 KB)

**7. Caste Proof (Non-Reserved Category)**
- Some states require proof that you belong to the General category and not SC/ST/OBC
- A general caste certificate or affidavit may be required

### Document Specifications for Online Upload

- **File Format:** PDF or JPEG/JPG
- **File Size:**
  - Photograph: Under 50 KB
  - Documents: 100 KB to 2 MB (varies by state)
- **Resolution:** Clear, readable, all corners visible
- **Self-Attestation:** All photocopies should be self-attested (signed by the applicant)

APPLICATION PROCESS - ONLINE
==================================================

### Step 1: Pre-Application Research

- Identify your state's e-District or citizen service portal
- Read the EWS Certificate application guidelines
- Check eligibility criteria, required documents, and fees

### Step 2: Register on e-District Portal

- Click "New User Registration" or "Citizen Registration"
- Enter personal details: Name, DOB, Mobile, Email, Aadhaar
- Create username and password
- Verify via OTP
- Complete registration

### Step 3: Login

- Go to portal homepage
- Click "Citizen Login"
- Enter username and password
- Enter captcha
- Click "Login"

### Step 4: Select EWS Certificate Service

- On dashboard, click "Apply for Services"
- Select "EWS Certificate" or "Income and Asset Certificate for Economically Weaker Section"
- Application form opens

### Step 5: Fill Application Form

**Personal Details:**
- Full name, father's name, mother's name
- Date of birth, gender
- Category: General
- Aadhaar number, mobile, email

**Address:**
- Complete permanent address with PIN code
- Current address (if different)

**Family Income Details:**
- Father's occupation and income
- Mother's occupation and income
- Spouse's occupation and income (if applicable)
- Other family members' income
- Total family income (must be below ₹8 lakh)

**Asset Details:**
- Agricultural land details (if any)
- Residential property details (flat/plot, area, location)
- Ensure all assets are within prescribed limits

**Declaration:**
- Read and confirm all statements
- Tick checkbox acknowledging penalties for false information

### Step 6: Upload Documents

- Aadhaar Card
- Income Proof (Income Certificate, salary slips, ITR)
- Address Proof / Domicile Certificate
- Land/Property records (if applicable)
- Self-Declaration Affidavit (Annexure-B)
- Passport-size Photograph
- PAN Card (if available)

### Step 7: Review and Submit

- Preview the entire application
- Verify all details and uploaded documents
- Correct any errors
- Submit the application
- Note the Application Reference Number / Acknowledgment Number
- Take screenshot

### Step 8: Payment

- Pay the application fee (if applicable)
- Fee: ₹10 to ₹500 (varies by state)
- Payment modes: Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, UPI
- Download payment receipt

### Step 9: Download Acknowledgment

- Download and print acknowledgment receipt
- Save PDF copy

### Step 10: Track Application Status

- Visit portal after 3-5 days
- Click "Track Application"
- Enter acknowledgment number
- Check status: Submitted / Under Verification / Approved / Rejected

### Step 11: Field Verification (if required)

- Revenue official (Patwari, Lekhpal) may visit your home
- Be present during visit
- Keep original documents ready
- Cooperate with the officer
- Sign verification report

### Step 12: Download EWS Certificate

- Once status shows "Approved" or "Certificate Ready"
- Click "Download Certificate"
- Enter acknowledgment number
- Download digitally signed PDF
- Print on A4 paper
- Verify all details
- Upload to DigiLocker for digital access

==================================================

### Step 1: Visit Revenue Office

**Office Locations:**

- Pay at cash counter
- Collect payment receipt

### Step 8: Field Verification

- Revenue officer may visit your home
- Be present
- Provide original documents
- Sign verification report

### Step 9: Collect Certificate

- You will be notified when ready (SMS/call/letter)
- Visit office to collect certificate
- Or receive by post
- Verify all details

==================================================
VALIDITY AND RENEWAL
==================================================

### Validity

- **EWS Certificate is valid for one financial year** (from April 1 to March 31).
- The certificate is valid across India for Central Government jobs and admissions.
- Some states may require a state-specific certificate for state-level benefits.

### Renewal

- The EWS Certificate must be **renewed annually**.
- After March 31st of each year, the old certificate expires.
- Follow the same application process for renewal.
- Provide updated income and asset proofs for the current financial year.
- Attach the old EWS Certificate to support faster processing.

==================================================
VERIFICATION AND AUTHENTICITY
==================================================

### How to Verify an EWS Certificate

**Online Verification:**
1. Visit the issuing state's e-District portal
2. Look for "Verify Certificate" option
3. Enter the certificate number and other details
4. If genuine, full details will be displayed

**Physical Verification:**
- Check for digital signature and official seal
- Verify certificate number format
- Check issuing authority's name and designation
- Cross-check with acknowledgment receipt

### Identifying Fake Certificates

**Red Flags:**
- No certificate number or invalid format
- No digital signature or seal
- Poor print quality
- Issuing authority name is incorrect
- Does not verify on official portal

**Penalty for Fake Certificate:**
- Criminal offense under Indian Penal Code
- Imprisonment and fine
- Cancellation of all benefits obtained

**Action:**
- Report to District Magistrate, Tehsildar, or local police

==================================================
USAGE OF EWS CERTIFICATE
==================================================

**Where Required:**
- **Central Government Jobs:** UPSC, SSC, Banking, Railways, etc.
- **State Government Jobs:** State PSCs, other state recruitment
- **Educational Institutions:**
  - Central Universities, IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS
  - State-run universities and colleges
- **Entrance Exams:** NEET, JEE, CAT, etc.

**What it Provides:**
- 10% reservation in admissions and job vacancies
- EWS candidates compete only with other EWS candidates for these reserved seats
- If EWS seats are not filled, they are usually transferred to the General category

==================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES
==================================================

### Age Relaxation

- **No age relaxation** is provided under the EWS category, unlike SC/ST/OBC.

### Minority Institutions

- **EWS reservation does not apply** to minority institutions (established and run by religious or linguistic minorities).

### Central vs. State Certificate Format

- The Central Government has a prescribed format (Annexure-I of DoPT guidelines).
- For Central Government jobs and admissions, this format is required.
- Some states issue certificates in their own format. Check if a Central format certificate is required for the specific application.

### DigiLocker

- Upload your EWS Certificate to DigiLocker for digital access.
- It is legally valid and accepted by most departments.
- No need to carry physical copy.

### Common Errors to Avoid

- Incorrectly calculating family income (include all sources)
- Misinterpreting asset limits (club all properties)
- Mismatch in names on different documents
- Submitting an expired Income Certificate
- Not getting the self-declaration affidavit notarized

CONCLUSION
==================================================

An EWS Certificate is a valuable document for availing 10% reservation benefits for the General category. The application process requires strict adherence to income and asset criteria. Applying online through your state's e-District portal is the fastest and most efficient method.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Verify eligibility thoroughly before applying
- Gather all income and asset documents
- Apply online for faster processing
- Renew the certificate annually
- Verify certificate authenticity

**Official Portals:**
- Visit your state's e-District portal (refer to the table in this document)
- For queries, contact Tehsildar's office or state helpline

**Remember:**
- Always use official government portals (.gov.in)
- Do not pay bribes or rely on middlemen
- EWS certificates are usually issued for a nominal fee
- Digital certificates are legally valid


==================================================
SECTION A: UNDERSTANDING ELIGIBILITY AND PREREQUISITES
==================================================

1. Confirm you are a citizen of India.
2. Check your date of birth.
3. Calculate your age as on January 1st of the current year.
4. Ensure you are 18 years of age or above on the qualifying date (January 1st).
5. Confirm you are not already enrolled in any electoral roll.
6. Check if you have been enrolled as a voter in any other constituency.
7. If already enrolled elsewhere, do not apply using Form 6.
8. If you are a service personnel, ensure you are not already enrolled as service elector.
9. Understand that Form 6 is only for first-time enrollment.
10. Confirm you ordinarily reside at the address you will mention in the form.

==================================================
SECTION B: GATHER REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==================================================

11. Collect proof of age documents.
12. Choose one from: Birth certificate, 10th mark sheet, school leaving certificate, PAN card, Aadhaar card, Passport, Driving License.
13. If you have birth certificate, keep it ready.
14. If you have 10th mark sheet showing date of birth, keep it ready.
15. If you have Aadhaar card, keep it ready.
16. If you have Passport, keep it ready.
17. Make a photocopy of the chosen age proof document.
18. Self-attest the photocopy by signing across the copy.
19. Collect proof of address documents.
20. Choose one from: Aadhaar card, Electricity bill (at least 1 year old), Water bill, Gas connection bill, Bank passbook, Passport, Ration card, Rent agreement (registered), Sale deed (if own house), Land owning records.
21. If the address proof is in your parent's or spouse's name, that is acceptable.
22. Ensure the address proof document shows your current residential address.
23. Make a photocopy of the address proof document.
24. Self-attest the photocopy by signing across it.
25. If you don't have any of the standard documents, collect any other document showing your address.
26. Note the name of the alternative document you will use.
27. If you are a student, decide whether to enroll at parent's address or hostel/mess address.
28. If homeless, understand that field verification will be done.
29. Collect Aadhaar card if available (optional but recommended).
30. Note your Aadhaar number if you have it.
31. Get a recent passport-size colour photograph taken.
32. Ensure photograph size is 4.5 cm X 3.5 cm.
33. Ensure photograph has white background.
34. Ensure your eyes are open in the photograph.
35. Ensure both edges of your face are clearly visible.
36. Ensure photograph is good quality and clear.
37. Do NOT sign on the photograph.
38. Get at least 2 copies of the photograph printed.
39. If you have disability, collect disability certificate.
40. Note the percentage of disability mentioned in the certificate.
41. Keep disability certificate ready to attach.

==================================================
SECTION C: FIND YOUR CONSTITUENCY DETAILS
==================================================

42. Find out your Assembly Constituency (AC) name and number.
43. Find out your Parliamentary Constituency (PC) name and number.
44. Visit the National Voters' Service Portal at www.nvsp.in.
45. Click on "Know Your Constituency" or "Search Electoral Roll".
46. Enter your address details.
47. The portal will show your AC and PC details.
48. Note down the AC number and name.
49. Note down the PC number and name.
50. Note down the name of the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO).
51. Note down the ERO office address.
52. If you are unable to find constituency details, don't worry; assistance will be provided.

==================================================
SECTION D: DOWNLOAD FORM 6
==================================================

53. Open a web browser on your computer or smartphone.
54. Visit the official National Voters' Service Portal: www.nvsp.in
55. On the homepage, look for "Forms" or "Download Forms" section.
56. Click on "Form 6 - Application for inclusion of name in electoral roll".
57. Download the Form 6 PDF file.
58. Save the file to your computer or phone.
59. Open the downloaded Form 6 PDF.
60. Read all instructions carefully.
61. Decide whether you will apply online or offline.

==================================================
SECTION E: FILL FORM 6 OFFLINE (MANUAL FILLING)
==================================================

62. If filling offline, print the Form 6 on A4 size paper.
63. Use black or blue pen to fill the form.
64. Write clearly in capital letters.
65. At the top, fill the name of the Electoral Registration Officer.
66. Fill the Assembly Constituency number and name (if known).
67. Fill the Parliamentary Constituency number and name (if known).
68. In Item (1): Write your full name in English.
69. Write your full name in the official language of the state (Hindi or regional language).
70. Ensure spelling is exact and matches your documents.
71. In Item (2a): Tick the appropriate relationship - Father / Mother / Husband / Others.
72. Write the name of your father, mother, or husband as applicable.
73. If married female, preferably mention husband's name.
74. In Item (2b): Write the surname of your relative.
75. In Item (3): Tick your marital status - Married / Unmarried.
76. In Item (4): Write your mobile number.
77. Write your email address if available.
78. In Item (5a): Write your Aadhaar number if you have it.
79. If you don't have Aadhaar, tick the box at Item 5(b) "I do not have Aadhaar".
80. In Item (6): Tick mark your gender - Male / Female / Third Gender.
81. Tick mark clearly in the appropriate box.
82. In Item (7a): Write your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
83. Write your age in years as on the qualifying date.
84. In Item (7b): Tick the box corresponding to the age proof document you are attaching.
85. If attaching another document not listed, tick "Any other document" and write its name.
86. In Item (8a): Write your complete current residential address.
87. Write House/Flat number.
88. Write Street/Road name.
89. Write Locality/Area name.
90. Write Town/City name.
91. Write District name.
92. Write State name.
93. Write PIN code (mandatory).
94. In Item (8b): Tick the box corresponding to the address proof document you are attaching.
95. Tick whether the document is in your name or parent's/spouse's/adult child's name.
96. If attaching another document not listed, write its name in the space provided.
97. In Item (9): If you have a disability, tick the category - Locomotive / Visual / Deaf & Dumb / Other.
98. Write description if "Other".
99. Write percentage of disability.
100. Tick "Yes" if you are attaching disability certificate.
101. If no disability, leave Item (9) blank.
102. In Item (10): If a family member is already enrolled at your address, fill their details.
103. Write the name of the family member.
104. Write your relationship with them.
105. Write their EPIC number if known.
106. If no family member enrolled, leave Item (10) blank.
107. In the DECLARATION section, read all statements carefully.
108. In Item (i): Write your place of birth - Village/Town, District, State/UT.
109. In Item (ii): Write since when you are ordinarily residing at the current address (month and year).
110. In Item (iii): Confirm you are applying for the first time and not enrolled elsewhere.
111. In Item (iv): If you don't have standard age proof documents, write the name of the alternative document.
112. If you have standard documents, strike off item (iv).
113. In Item (v): Read the statement about punishment for false declaration.
114. Write the date of filling the form.
115. Write the place where you are filling the form.
116. Sign in the space provided.
117. If unable to sign, put your left hand thumb impression.
118. If you are a person with intellectual disability, your legal guardian can sign.
119. Paste the unsigned passport-size photograph in the space provided on the form.
120. Ensure photograph is pasted properly and clearly visible.

==================================================
SECTION F: ATTACH DOCUMENTS TO FORM 6
==================================================

121. Take the self-attested photocopy of age proof document.
122. Attach it to Form 6 using a pin or stapler.
123. Take the self-attested photocopy of address proof document.
124. Attach it to Form 6.
125. If attaching disability certificate, attach its photocopy.
126. Ensure all attachments are secure and will not fall off.
127. Arrange documents in order: Form 6, photograph, age proof, address proof, disability certificate (if any).
128. Do not fold documents excessively.
129. Keep the filled Form 6 and attachments in a clean envelope or folder.

==================================================
SECTION G: SUBMIT FORM 6 OFFLINE
==================================================

130. Find the nearest Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) office.
131. Note the ERO office address and working hours.
132. Working hours are usually 10 AM to 5 PM on working days.
133. Visit the ERO office during working hours.
134. Carry the filled Form 6 with all attachments.
135. Carry original documents for verification (age proof, address proof, Aadhaar, disability certificate).
136. At the ERO office, look for the "New Voter Registration" or "Form 6 Submission" counter.
137. Wait for your turn if there is a queue.
138. When called, approach the officer politely.
139. Tell the officer you want to submit Form 6 for new voter registration.
140. Hand over the filled Form 6 with attachments.
141. Hand over original documents for verification.
142. Officer will check your form and documents.
143. Answer any questions asked by the officer.
144. Officer may ask you to confirm your details.
145. If everything is in order, officer will accept your form.
146. Officer will give you an acknowledgment receipt.
147. The acknowledgment will have an acknowledgment number and date.
148. Note down the acknowledgment number carefully.
149. Keep the acknowledgment receipt safely.
150. Ask the officer about the expected timeline for processing.
151. Ask about field verification if applicable.
152. Thank the officer and leave.

==================================================
SECTION H: APPLY ONLINE THROUGH NVSP PORTAL
==================================================

153. Open a web browser on your computer or smartphone.
154. Visit www.nvsp.in (National Voters' Service Portal).
155. On the homepage, look for "Apply Online for Registration of New Voter" or "Form 6".
156. Click on it.
157. You will see the online Form 6 application page.
158. Read all instructions on the page.
159. Click "Fill the form".
160. Select the state where you reside.
161. Select the district.
162. Select the Assembly Constituency (AC).
163. If you don't know your AC, use the "Search AC" option by entering your address.
164. Proceed to fill the form.
165. In "Applicant Details" section, enter your full name in English.
166. Enter your full name in the state's official language (system may auto-transliterate).
167. Select relationship type: Father / Mother / Husband / Others.
168. Enter the name of your father/mother/husband.
169. Enter surname.
170. Select marital status.
171. Enter your mobile number.
172. Enter your email address.
173. Enter your Aadhaar number if you have it.
174. If no Aadhaar, tick the checkbox "I do not possess Aadhaar Number".
175. Select your gender: Male / Female / Third Gender.
176. Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
177. Your age will be calculated automatically.
178. Select the document you are using as age proof from the dropdown.
179. If using another document, select "Any other document" and enter its name.
180. In "Address Details" section, enter your complete current residential address.
181. Enter House/Flat number.
182. Enter Street/Road name.
183. Enter Locality/Area.
184. Enter Town/City.
185. Enter District.
186. Enter State.
187. Enter PIN code (mandatory).
188. Select the document you are using as address proof from the dropdown.
189. Indicate if the document is in your name or in parent's/spouse's/adult child's name.
190. If using another document, select "Any other document" and enter its name.
191. In "Disability Details" section (optional), select category of disability if applicable.
192. Enter percentage of disability if applicable.
193. Indicate if you are attaching disability certificate.
194. In "Family Member Details" section (optional), enter name of family member already enrolled at your address.
195. Enter relationship with that family member.
196. Enter their EPIC number if known.
197. In "Upload Documents" section, click "Choose File" for photograph.
198. Select the scanned/digital copy of your passport-size photograph.
199. Ensure file format is JPEG or PNG.
200. Ensure file size is within the limit (usually 50-100 KB).
201. Upload the photograph.
202. Click "Choose File" for age proof document.
203. Select the scanned copy of your self-attested age proof.
204. Ensure file is clear and readable.
205. Upload the age proof document.
206. Click "Choose File" for address proof document.
207. Select the scanned copy of your self-attested address proof.
208. Upload the address proof document.
209. If applicable, upload disability certificate.
210. Review all uploaded documents on screen.
211. In "Declaration" section, read all statements carefully.
212. Enter your place of birth (Village/Town, District, State/UT).
213. Enter since when you are ordinarily residing at the current address (month and year).
214. Tick the checkboxes confirming the declaration statements.
215. Tick the checkbox "I am aware of the punishment for false declaration".
216. Enter the date of submission.
217. Enter the place.
218. If the portal requires captcha, enter the captcha code.
219. Click "Preview" to review your entire application.
220. Check all details carefully.
221. If any error, click "Edit" and correct it.
222. If all details are correct, click "Submit".
223. The system will generate an acknowledgment.
224. An acknowledgment number will be displayed on screen.
225. Note down the acknowledgment number.
226. Take a screenshot of the acknowledgment.
227. Download the acknowledgment receipt PDF if available.
228. Print the acknowledgment receipt.
229. Save the acknowledgment number and receipt safely.
230. You may receive a confirmation SMS and email on your registered mobile and email.

==================================================
SECTION I: AFTER SUBMISSION - TRACKING AND FOLLOW-UP
==================================================

231. After submitting Form 6, wait for processing.
232. Processing usually takes 4-6 weeks.
233. Field verification may be conducted by ERO or designated officer.
234. If field verification is required, cooperate with the visiting officer.
235. Provide original documents for verification.
236. Track your application status online.
237. Visit www.nvsp.in.
238. Click on "Track Application Status".
239. Enter your acknowledgment number or reference ID.
240. Enter other required details (name, date of birth, etc.).
241. Click "Track Status".
242. The status will be displayed: Pending / Under Verification / Approved / Rejected.
243. If status is "Approved", your name has been added to the electoral roll.
244. Your EPIC (Voter ID Card) will be printed and dispatched.
245. EPIC will be sent to your registered postal address by speed post.
246. Delivery usually takes 1-2 weeks after approval.
247. EPIC will be delivered free of cost under proper acknowledgment.
248. Receive and sign for the EPIC delivery.
249. Check all details on the EPIC card immediately upon receipt.
250. Verify your name, father's/husband's name, address, date of birth, photograph, and EPIC number.
251. If any error, apply for correction using Form 8.
252. If application is rejected, check the reason online or contact ERO.
253. Correct the errors and reapply.
254. Keep your EPIC card safely.
255. Make a photocopy of your EPIC for records.
256. You can also download your e-EPIC (digital Voter ID) from NVSP portal after enrollment.

1. What is a Driving Licence in India?

A Driving Licence is an official document that legally permits you to drive a motor vehicle on Indian roads. It is issued after passing both theory and practical driving tests.
2. Is a Learner's Licence mandatory before getting a Driving Licence?

Yes. You must obtain a Learner's Licence for the relevant vehicle class and hold it for at least 30 days before applying for a permanent Driving Licence.
3. What is the minimum age to apply for a Driving Licence?

    16 years for motorcycles without gear (up to 50cc)
    18 years for motorcycles with gear and light motor vehicles
    18-20 years for commercial vehicles (varies by state)

4. What documents are required to apply for a Driving Licence?

    Valid Learner's Licence (mandatory)
    Proof of address
    Proof of age
    Passport-size photographs
    Form 4 (application form)
    Medical certificates (if applicable)

5. Can I apply for a Driving Licence online?

Yes. Most states offer online application through their RTO websites and the Parivahan portal.
6. What is the application fee for a Driving Licence?

The application fee is Rs. 200.
7. How long is a Driving Licence valid?

    For non-transport vehicles: 20 years or until age 50, whichever is earlier
    For transport vehicles: 3 years initially, then renewable every 5 years
    For holders above 50 years: Renewable every 5 years

8. What is the driving test procedure?

The driving test assesses your practical driving skills, including vehicle control, basic maneuvers (parking, reverse, turns), road sense, and adherence to traffic rules.
9. What happens if I fail the driving test?

You can retake the test after 7 days. You may need to pay a retest fee (varies by state).
10. Can I drive alone with a Learner's Licence?

No. With a Learner's Licence, you must always be accompanied by a person holding a valid permanent driving licence for the same vehicle class.
11. Do I need to display an 'L' sign with a Learner's Licence?

Yes. Displaying an 'L' sign is mandatory while driving with a Learner's Licence.
12. Can I apply for both motorcycle and car licences together?

Yes, but you need separate Learner's Licences for each class and must pass driving tests for both.
13. Is my Driving Licence valid throughout India?

Yes. A Driving Licence issued in any state is valid throughout India.
14. How do I renew my Driving Licence?

Apply for renewal online or offline using Form 9, submit medical certificates if required, and pay the renewal fee of Rs. 250.
15. Can I renew an expired Driving Licence?

Yes. You can renew within 1 year after expiry. Beyond that, check with your RTO as you may need to apply afresh.
16. What should I do if my Driving Licence is lost?

Apply for a duplicate licence immediately. Submit LLD form, FIR copy, and required documents, and pay Rs. 200.
17. How do I apply for an International Driving Licence?

Submit Form 4A with passport, visa, flight ticket, Indian driving licence, and photographs. Pay the fee and collect the IDP.
18. How long is an International Driving Licence valid?

1 year from the date of issue.
19. Can I add a new vehicle class to my existing Driving Licence?

Yes. Obtain a Learner's Licence for the new class, hold it for 30 days, pass the driving test, and pay Rs. 50.
20. What forms are required for a Driving Licence?

    Form 4: Application for Driving Licence
    Form 1: Medical Certificate
    Form 1A: Physical Fitness Certificate
    Form 9: Renewal application
    LLD Form: Duplicate licence application
    Form 4A: International Driving Licence application

All forms are available on state RTO websites and the Parivahan portal.
21. Is a medical certificate required for a Driving Licence?

Yes, for commercial vehicle licences and for applicants above a certain age (usually 40 or 50, varies by state).
22. What is Form 4?

Form 4 is the application form for obtaining a new Driving Licence. It contains personal details, vehicle class, and declarations.
23. What is Form 1?

Form 1 is a Medical Certificate that must be signed by a registered medical practitioner.
24. What is Form 1A?

Form 1A is a Physical Fitness Certificate required for commercial and transport vehicle licence applicants.
25. Can I check my Driving Licence application status online?

Yes. Visit the Parivahan portal or your state RTO website and check status using your application reference number.
26. Can I download my Driving Licence online?

Yes. Once issued, you can download a digital copy from the Parivahan portal using your DL number and date of birth.
27. What should I do if there is an error in my Driving Licence?

Visit your RTO with proof of correct details and apply for a correction/modification.
28. Can a commercial vehicle driver apply for a non-transport licence?

Yes. You can hold licences for multiple vehicle classes.
29. Is an 8th standard pass certificate required for all Driving Licences?

No. It is required only for commercial and transport vehicle licences.
30. Can I drive a motorcycle with a car licence?

No. You must have a separate licence for motorcycles.
31. Can I drive a car with a motorcycle licence?

No. You must have a Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) licence to drive a car.
32. What is the difference between LMV-NT and LMV-TR?

    LMV-NT: Light Motor Vehicle - Non-Transport (for personal use)
    LMV-TR: Light Motor Vehicle - Transport (for commercial use)

33. Can I apply for a Driving Licence in a different state?

Generally, you should apply in the state where you reside. You may need proof of residence in that state.
34. Is it necessary to attend the RTO in person for the driving test?

Yes. You must be physically present for the practical driving test.
35. Can I use my Learner's Licence as ID proof?

It depends on the context. For official purposes like boarding flights, some organizations may not accept LL; a permanent driving licence or other government IDs are preferred.
36. What is the validity of a Learner's Licence?

6 months from the date of issue.
37. Can I renew my Learner's Licence?

No. If your Learner's Licence expires, you must apply for a fresh one.
38. How soon after getting a Learner's Licence can I apply for a Driving Licence?

You must wait for at least 30 days after the issuance of your Learner's Licence.
39. Can I practice driving on highways with a Learner's Licence?

Yes, but only under the supervision of a licensed driver and following all learner's licence restrictions (displaying 'L' sign, etc.).
40. What is the process for converting a foreign driving licence to an Indian licence?

You typically need to apply at the RTO with your foreign licence, passport, visa, and other documents. The process varies; check with your local RTO.
41. Can I drive a vehicle rented/hired with my Driving Licence?

Yes, as long as the vehicle class matches your licence.
42. Do I need insurance to get a Driving Licence?

No. However, vehicle insurance is mandatory to drive on public roads.
43. What is the penalty for driving without a valid Driving Licence?

Fines up to Rs. 5,000 and/or imprisonment up to 3 months, plus vehicle impoundment.
44. Can I get a Driving Licence if I have a criminal record?

Licensing authorities may refuse or revoke a licence for certain offenses. Disclosure may be required.
45. Can persons with disabilities get a Driving Licence?

Yes. Special provisions exist for persons with disabilities. Medical fitness certificates and adapted vehicles may be required.
46. What is a Transport Driving Licence?

A licence authorizing the holder to drive commercial vehicles for hire or reward (taxis, trucks, buses).
47. What is the difference between MC 50CC and FVG?

    MC 50CC: Motorcycles with engine capacity of 50cc or less
    FVG: Motorcycles of any capacity but without gears (scooters, mopeds)

48. Can I apply for a Driving Licence if I am illiterate?

Yes. Illiterate individuals can apply. The test may be conducted orally or with visual aids.
49. What should I do if my Driving Licence is suspended?

You cannot drive during suspension. Follow RTO instructions for reinstatement, which may include attending hearings or completing training.
50. Can I transfer my Driving Licence to another state?

Driving licences are valid throughout India. If you move to another state, you may need to update your address on the licence by applying for a change of address at the RTO in your new state.


==================================================
SECTION A: PRE-APPLICATION PREPARATION (ELIGIBILITY AUDIT)
==================================================

1. Understand what an EWS Certificate is and its purpose.
2. Confirm you belong to the General (unreserved) category.
3. Ensure you do NOT have an SC, ST, or OBC certificate.
4. If you hold any reserved category certificate, you are NOT eligible for EWS.
5. Calculate your total gross annual family income from all sources.
6. Include income from: salary, agriculture, business, profession, rent, interest, dividends, pension.
7. Include income of: yourself, parents, spouse, siblings below 18, and children below 18.
8. Do NOT include income of married siblings.
9. Ensure total family income is below ₹8 lakh per annum.
10. If income is ₹8 lakh or more, you are NOT eligible.
11. Check your family's agricultural land ownership.
12. Calculate total agricultural land across all locations.
13. Ensure total agricultural land is less than 5 acres.
14. If 5 acres or more, you are NOT eligible.
15. Check ownership of residential flats.
16. Measure the total area of any residential flat owned by the family.
17. Ensure the residential flat is less than 1,000 sq. ft.
18. If 1,000 sq. ft. or more, you are NOT eligible.
19. Check ownership of residential plots.
20. Determine if the plot is in a notified municipality or non-notified area.
21. Notified municipalities: urban areas officially recognized by the government.
22. Non-notified areas: rural or semi-urban areas.
23. If plot is in a notified municipality, ensure it is less than 100 sq. yards.
24. If plot is in a non-notified area, ensure it is less than 200 sq. yards.
25. If plot exceeds these limits, you are NOT eligible.
26. Confirm you meet ALL eligibility criteria: General category, income <₹8 lakh, assets within limits.
27. If ALL criteria are met, proceed with the application.

SECTION B: GATHER REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==================================================

28. Collect your Aadhaar Card (mandatory).
29. Make a clear photocopy of Aadhaar Card (front and back).
30. Scan Aadhaar Card in PDF or JPEG format.
31. Ensure scan is clear, under 200 KB to 2 MB.
32. Collect PAN Card (optional but recommended for income verification).
33. Make a photocopy of PAN Card.
34. Scan PAN Card.
35. Collect Income Proof documents.
36. For salaried employees: Collect salary slips (last 6 months) and Form 16.
37. For self-employed/business owners: Collect Income Tax Return (ITR) of last financial year.
38. For agricultural income: Collect land revenue records showing income.
39. For pensioners: Collect pension payment order or bank statement.
40. For multiple income sources: Collect proof for all sources.
41. Make photocopies of all income documents.
42. Scan all income documents.
43. If you have an Income Certificate issued by Tehsildar, collect it (highly recommended).
44. The Income Certificate should show family income below ₹8 lakh.
45. Collect Domicile Certificate or Address Proof.
46. Choose one: Domicile Certificate, Ration Card, Voter ID, Electricity bill, Water bill, Rent agreement.
47. Ensure address proof is recent (within last 6 months if utility bill).
48. Make a photocopy of address proof.
49. Scan address proof.
50. Collect land records (if you own agricultural land).
51. Collect 7/12 extract, revenue records, or land ownership documents.
52. Ensure land records show total land is less than 5 acres.
53. Make photocopies of land records.
54. Scan land records.
55. Collect property documents (if you own residential property).
56. Collect sale deed, property tax receipts, or municipal records.
57. Ensure documents show property is within asset limits.
58. Make photocopies of property documents.
59. Scan property documents.
60. Collect passport-size photographs (3-4 recent color photographs).
61. Scan one passport-size photograph (JPEG, under 50 KB).
62. Prepare Self-Declaration Affidavit (Annexure-B format).
63. Download Annexure-B format from UPSC website or state revenue portal.
64. Or buy stamp paper (₹10 to ₹20 depending on state).
65. Draft the affidavit declaring:
    - Your name, father's name, date of birth
    - You belong to General category
    - Your family income is below ₹8 lakh
    - Your family assets are within limits
    - All information is true and correct
66. Get the affidavit notarized or signed before a magistrate.
67. Make a photocopy of the affidavit.
68. Scan the notarized affidavit.
69. If your state requires a Caste/Non-Reserved Category Certificate, collect it.
70. Some states require proof that you are NOT SC/ST/OBC.
71. Keep all documents organized in a folder (physical and digital).
